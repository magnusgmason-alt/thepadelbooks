How to find padel players near me

6 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 8 Nov 2026

If you search "find padel players near me", the fastest answers are booking apps with open matches, your local club's social sessions and americanos, and local WhatsApp or Facebook groups. Each suits a different player: apps for filling a court tonight, club sessions for meeting regulars, leagues for competitive games. This guide explains how each option works, which apps do what, and how to find players at the right level.

Booking apps that help you find padel players

Which app is best depends on which one the clubs near you use, so check your local club first. Here's what the main ones do, based on their own descriptions and help pages.

Playtomic

Playtomic is the app most players meet first. Padel Browser's 2026 app round-up says the overwhelming majority of US padel clubs run their court reservations through it. For finding players, the key feature is open matches:

  • In the "Find a match" section you filter by sport, location and time, then join an open match or create your own, according to Playtomic's help pages.
  • Players are matched with opponents of a similar level. If your level is outside the match's range, you can request a spot, and it only opens to you if all the players accept.
  • Joining a public match means paying your share online by card or club wallet.
  • The match creator can restrict it to men, women or mixed teams, or leave it open to all.
  • A "Community Match" lets you organise a game on a court that isn't a Playtomic club, and make it public so others can find and join. The organiser books and pays the facility directly; Playtomic says the creator is solely responsible for the booking and payments.

MATCHi

MATCHi is a court booking app from Gothenburg, Sweden, covering padel and other racket sports. Its App Store description lists booking courts, inviting friends and splitting payments, and a Venue Communities feature to find players to play with at your venue.

Padel Mates

Padel Mates describes itself as a way to book and pay for courts and activities, set your game level and favourite facilities, and get personalised match suggestions based on your skill level. It also lists games, tournaments and training sessions at your chosen centres. Padel Browser notes that it's strong in some cities and thin in others, which is true of most apps: they're only as good as the clubs and players using them nearby.

Check before you download

Apps add and change features often. We've described only what each app's own pages say in November 2026. Look up your nearest club and see which app it takes bookings through; that's usually where the local players are.

Club social sessions and americanos

Most clubs run social formats where you sign up on your own and get mixed in with other players. The americano is the classic: partners rotate every round, so in one evening you play with and against most of the group. A mexicano re-draws the pairings from the standings, which helps when levels vary. Both are brilliant for meeting regulars, and you'll usually leave with a few phone numbers. Our guide to padel games for groups lists more formats.

Group coaching

LTA Padel points out that group coaching sessions are a great way to find people to play with outside lessons. You meet players at your level who want to improve, which makes them ideal regular partners. See our guide to padel lessons for what to expect.

Leagues and competitions

Box leagues, ladders and club tournaments give you a steady stream of matches against players of similar standard. In Britain, LTA Padel says competition organisers will try to find you a partner if you don't have one, and suggests beginner and intermediate players look at Grade 5 (Local Tour) or Grade 4 (County Tour) events. Elsewhere, ask your club or national federation what runs locally. Our guides to tournament formats and your first padel tournament explain how they work.

WhatsApp and Facebook groups

Many local padel communities live in chat groups. LTA Padel notes that many online social groups are run by players for players and suggests searching social media for padel groups in your area. Padel Browser mentions WhatsApp groups as a common way to find pick-up games at clubs in New York and Miami.

How to get the most from them:

  • Search Facebook for "padel" plus your town or club name, and ask at the club reception about their WhatsApp groups.
  • Introduce yourself with your level, the days and times you can play, and which clubs you can get to.
  • Reply quickly to "need a fourth" messages; spots go in minutes.
  • Turn up on time and pay your share promptly. That's how you get invited again.

Find players at the right level

A game that's far too hard or too easy is no fun for anyone. Be honest about your level when you join open matches and groups. Our guide to padel levels explained covers the common scales, including app ratings. If you're brand new, an intro session or a beginner americano is kinder than an open match with strangers; our guide to your first time playing padel walks you through it.

Once you've found a group, a little preparation helps you hold your place in it. The Padel Playbook: Beginner & Intermediate covers the positions and wall play that make you a reliable fourth, and The Padel Drill Book gives you drills to run when you meet a partner for practice rather than a match.

Where to find courts in the first place

If you don't yet know where to play, start with court finders. In Britain, LTA Padel's court finder lets you search by postcode, town or city and view each venue's court details and booking information. Booking apps also show nearby clubs on a map. Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to find padel players?

The one your local clubs use. Playtomic is the most widespread and has open matches filtered by level; MATCHi and Padel Mates also help you book and find players where clubs use them.

How do I find people to play padel with if I'm a beginner?

Join a beginner group session or a club americano, where you're mixed in with others at your level. Once you've played a few times, try open matches on an app within your level range.

Can I join a padel match on my own?

Yes. Open matches on booking apps and club social sessions are designed for single players. You pay your share and the app or organiser fills the other spots.

What if my level is outside an open match's range?

On Playtomic you can request a spot, and it only opens to you if all the players accept. Be honest about your level so the game stays good for everyone.

Are there padel WhatsApp groups near me?

Very likely. Ask at your club's reception and search social media for padel plus your town. Many groups are run by players for players and post last-minute spots.
