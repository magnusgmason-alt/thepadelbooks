## padel-injuries (checked against Dahmen et al. 2023)
1. Elbow range "10% to 74%; most common site in every study": wrong. 74.4% is a per-player figure from a different denominator, and the 2015 junior group had low back (23%) above elbow (10%). Changed to "about 10% to 37%; the joint the review found most often reported". FAQ and lede reworded the same way.
2. Elbow paragraph: "most frequently reported single diagnosis… 74.4%" was misleading. Now: the main elbow diagnosis, 20.5% of injuries in one study, and a larger share in a study counting per player.
3. Knee range "3% to 23%": the review gives 10%–22.9%. Fixed in the table and the FAQ.
4. Calf/leg range "8% to 14%": the review includes 22.1% tennis leg, so 8%–22%. Fixed. Tennis leg is now described as a share "by body region", as the paper reports it.
5. Foot row: added the 11.45% foot figure (García-Fernández).
6. Tendon claim: now "three of the four studies that reported injury type".
7. Incidence: added that the authors say it rests on limited data (one study).
8. "Order of business: elbow first": softened. Two studies found the lower limbs as a whole above the upper limbs.
9. "No padel-specific prevention trials exist yet": overstated. Now "the review found no studies testing padel-specific prevention programmes" (body).
10. FAQ prevalence "previous year": corrected to "previous 8 to 12 months".
11. Rocamora-López 2025: "sand-filled courts / certain sole types" made specific. Mixed or herringbone soles go with muscle, tendon and ligament injuries; sand courts with muscle and ligament injuries; more match hours with more injuries.
12. NHS knee: "most knee pain gets better within a few days" changed to "you should start to feel better in a few days" (NHS 111 Wales wording).
13. IOC: aligned with the abstract ("emerging evidence indicates poor load management is a major risk factor").
14. Lauersen: "to around a third" changed to "to less than a third" (RR 0.315). "Two or three times a week" was unsourced and became "regularly".
15. Gothenburg: "seeing more" changed to "several clinics seeing a rise". The 90% figure is attributed to the university rather than to "studies they cited" (no specific study named).
Verified: 8 studies, 2,022 players; 3/1,000 h and 8/1,000 matches; 40–72% and 60–95%; shoulder 7–34%; lower back 9–28%; ankle 7–10%; gastrocnemius 6.8%; plantar fasciitis 4–8%; overhead loading as the explanation; Spain, recall and definition limits; 216 players, cross-sectional; 3 cases needing surgery; 130 km/h; up to 90%; nidirect shoulder advice (2 weeks, 4–6 weeks, urgent signs); NHS knee advice (20 min every 2–3 h, a few weeks, urgent signs).

Removed as unverifiable: Moco "big points" usage; the HEAD verbatim quote; the "backhand side" contrapared example; "moves away from the opponents"; "two or three times a week".
