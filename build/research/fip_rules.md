# FIP Rules of Padel — detailed paraphrase (source: https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel.pdf), fetched 2026-10-23
NOTE: paraphrase, not verbatim. Do not quote as verbatim text. Cite rule numbers.

## Rule 1 Scoring
15/30/40/game; deuce at 40-40; advantage. Set: first to 6 games with 2-game margin; tie-break at 6-6. Best of three sets. Scoring options: Advantage, Star Point, Golden Point (single deciding point at deuce). Alternatives: 4-game mini set, 7-point match tie-break, 10-point super tie-break, each with 2-point margin.

## Rule 2 Times
Pair not ready 10 min after start loses by walkover (unless umpire decides otherwise). 3-minute courtesy rally (warm-up) before play. Play continuous: max 20 seconds between points; no food/drink between points. Change of ends: up to 90 seconds. End of set: up to 120 seconds. Tie-break side changes: 20 seconds. Clothing/equipment problems: reasonable time. Warm-up after suspension: none up to 5 min; 1 min for 5–20 min; 3 min if over 20 min. Suspended match resumes exactly where it stopped. Injury/treatable medical condition: 3-minute treatment, may be repeated at the next two changes of ends; once per condition. Accident/medical event not caused by play: up to 15 min at referee's discretion. Unusual incidents: up to 5 min recovery.

## Rule 3 Positions
Server and receiver diagonally opposite; partners may stand anywhere on their own side.

## Rule 4 Choice of sides/service
Coin toss; winner chooses serve/receive, side, or lets opponents choose.

## Rule 5 Change of ends
After 1st, 3rd and every subsequent odd game; in tie-break every 6 points. Mistake corrected when discovered; earlier points stand.

## Rule 6 Service
6.1 Server stands with one foot behind the service line, within the service box area (between extension of central line and side wall), stays there until serve is struck.
6.2 Ball bounced on the ground inside server's own box (behind service line) before being struck.
6.3 Server may not touch the service line or the imaginary central line with either foot.
6.4 At contact the ball must be at or below waist height; at least one foot in contact with the ground.
6.5 Serve diagonal into receiver's box, landing within its lines; start each game from the right side; alternate boxes.
6.6 Any contact or intentional attempt to hit counts as a serve (a miss = fault, 7b).
6.7 Wrong side: corrected when noticed; points played stand; a first-serve fault still counts.
6.8 Serve order chosen before each set; fixed for the set.
6.10 server must wait until receiver is ready (receiver must adapt to server's reasonable rhythm).

## Rule 7 Service faults
a) breaks Rule 6.1–6.6; b) misses the ball; c) ball lands outside receiver's box (lines are good); d) ball hits server, partner or their equipment; e) ball lands in box and touches the METAL FENCE (mesh) before second bounce = fault (glass is fine); f) ball lands in box and leaves through a gate on a court without safety zone.
Two consecutive faults = point lost (13q).

## Rule 8 Return of serve
8.1 receiver must let the ball bounce in receiving box and hit it before second bounce (no volleying the serve).
8.2 in first game of each set receiving pair decides who receives first; that player receives first serve of every game in the set.
8.3 receive alternately; order fixed for set/tie-break.
8.4 if order altered by mistake, continue until end of that game/tie-break, then revert.
8.5 if serve hits receiver or their racket before bouncing: point to server.

## Rule 9 Net / let on serve
9.1a "net": serve touches net or posts then lands in correct box, provided it doesn't touch the metal fence before second bounce -> repeated.
9.1b also "net" if after touching net/posts it hits a player or their equipment.
9.2a let if receiver not ready.
9.2b let on first serve: whole point replayed, two serves.
9.2c net or let on second serve: only second serve repeated.
(There is no limit on number of net serves mentioned.)

## Rule 10 Let point (point replayed)
a) ball splits; b) foreign object enters court; c) interruption from unexpected circumstances unrelated to players; d) ball hits an object on opponents' side (e.g. another ball) and that object moves so it could interfere/endanger; e) player must tell umpire immediately — if play continues, right to let lost; f) if let request invalid, player loses the point.

## Rule 11 Interference (hindrance)
Hindering an opponent executing a shot. Deliberate: point to opponent. Involuntary: let. Repeat involuntary by same pair: point to opponents.

## Rule 12 Ball in play
Pairs hit alternately. In play from valid serve until let or point decided. After bouncing on opponent's ground ball may touch any part of the court and stays in play; must be returned before second bounce. Parts of court: inside of walls, metal mesh fence (incl. its frame), ground, net, posts.

## Rule 13 Point lost
a) player/racket/anything worn or carried touches net, posts, tension cable or any part of opponent's court while ball in play.
b) exception: when out-of-court play authorised, door dividing vertical post above 0.92 m is neutral zone, any player may touch/hold it.
c) ball bounces twice before return.
d) out-of-court play NOT authorised: ball after bouncing crosses marked outer limit (side or end) or passes through gate -> point lost (for the side that had to return it).
e) out-of-court play authorised: ball after correct bounce goes over END wall -> point lost (can't be played); over SIDE wall or through door -> lost only if it bounces a second time or touches an element unrelated to the court.
f) player hits the ball before it has crossed the net.
g) player returns ball (directly or off own walls) and, without bouncing, it strikes opponent's walls, metal fence, or any element not related to/on the opponent's court.
h) return hits net or posts and then directly strikes opponent's wall, fence or unrelated element.
i) double hit.
j) after the player hits the ball, it touches the player, partner, or anything they wear.
k) ball hit by opponents touches any part of a player's body/equipment other than the racket (even if standing outside court/ball going out).
l) player hits ball and it touches the metal fence (mesh) or ground of own side or unrelated element on own side. (Hitting own GLASS walls is allowed — 14b.)
m) ball hit with a thrown racket.
n) any player jumps over the net during the point.
o) both partners hit the ball (simultaneously or one after the other). Exception: both try at once and one hits ball while other hits partner's racket — not a double hit.
p) hitter has one foot, both feet or any body part outside the court, unless out-of-court play authorised.
q) two consecutive service faults.
r) breaking the safety cord or dropping the racket: pair immediately loses the point.

## Rule 14 Correct return
a) opponents volley it, or it touches opponents' body, clothing or racket.
b) ball bounces directly in opponent's court, or hits a wall of player's OWN court first and then bounces in opponent's court (own-wall play is legal; own mesh is not, see 13l).
c) ball bounces in opponent's court then hits fence or wall.
d) ball bounces in opponent's court then leaves court by force/direction of shot hitting ceiling, lights or other object unrelated to court — correct return (point for hitter).
e) ball touches net/posts and lands in opponent's court.
f) ball hits object on opponent's ground not part of court (e.g. another ball) — valid.
g) ball bounces in opponent's court, comes back over to hitter's side (spin/wind) and opponent plays it there: valid ONLY if the opponent doesn't touch net, posts or the other court with body, clothing or racket, and conditions a–f met. (i.e. may reach over but not touch net/opponent court.)
h) not a double hit if contact is in same movement and ball's natural exit not substantially changed.
i) ball bouncing in the corner where wall meets ground in opponent's court: valid.
j) ball hit from outside the court (authorised out-of-court play) valid if other conditions met.

## Rule 15 Point won
a) after bouncing in opponent's court, ball leaves through a hole in the mesh or gets stuck in it.
b) after bouncing in opponent's court, ball stuck on flat top of the wall.

## Rule 16 Out-of-court play only when court and safety area meet specifications.
## Rule 17 Change of balls: announced in advance; after odd number of games, at start of set, or as specified.

## Equipment
Racket: max length 45.5 cm; handle max 20 cm; head max width 26 cm, max thickness 38 mm; holes 9–13 mm; non-elastic safety cord max 35 cm, worn on wrist — mandatory. Ball: 6.35–6.77 cm, 56.0–59.4 g; bounce 135–145 cm from 2.54 m; pressure 4.6–5.2 kg/2.54 cm².
Court: 20 x 10 m; service lines 6.95 m from net; net 0.88 m centre, 0.92 m ends.

## NOT ADDRESSED in FIP rules (do NOT invent a rule): two-handed grip / switching hands (no prohibition found); reaching over the net (only touching net/opponent court and jumping are covered; 14g covers ball spinning back); ball crossing outside posts; walking during serve beyond 6.1–6.4.

## CORRECTIONS from primary-source fact-check (2026-10-23) — these override anything above
- Rule 3: the receiver can be anywhere on their side, as can their partner and the server's partner. "Diagonal" only defines which opponent receives.
- Rule 6.1: server has at least ONE FOOT behind the service line, inside the service box, until the ball is served.
- Rule 6.2: the ball may not cross the service line or imaginary central line before being struck (else 7a fault).
- Rule 6.11: once the receiver attempts to return, "not ready" can't be claimed; a fault can't be called on a serve struck while the receiver was not ready.
- Rule 8.5: serve touching EITHER receiving player or their rackets before bouncing = point to server.
- Rule 11: the pair's SECOND involuntary interference loses the point.
- Rule 13k: text has no "even if outside/going out" wording (no exception exists, but don't quote it as text).
- Rule 13l: unrelated element "located on the ground" on own side only.
- Rule 14d: defines a correct return; doesn't itself award the point (Rule 15 only covers fence holes / top of wall).
- Rule 14g: no mention of spin/wind; applies to any ball that bounces correctly and returns to the hitter's side.
- Rule 2: umpire may allow up to 5 minutes' recovery e.g. for a player hit by the ball (unusual incidents).
- Etiquette section: bans hitting the ball at opponents when not in play; aggressive/unsportsmanlike behaviour treated severely.
