How many calories does padel burn?

4 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 17 Nov 2026

A typical hour of club padel burns roughly 350 to 550 calories for most adults. Heavier players, harder matches and long rallies push that up; social doubles with lots of chat between points brings it down. Here's where those numbers come from, and how to estimate your own.

The short answer

The Aspetar sports science review of padel (Ellis and colleagues, 2023) uses an approximate figure of 500 kcal per hour for a 70 kg player, and is clear that it's a generic assumption rather than a precise measurement. Real numbers depend on your weight, your level, how competitive the match is and how much of the hour is actually spent playing.

Calories per hour by body weight

Padel isn't yet listed in the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities, the standard reference for the energy cost of activities. The closest entries give doubles tennis 4.5 to 6.0 METs, general racquetball 7.0 and general squash 7.3. Using the standard estimate (calories per hour ≈ METs × body weight in kg), with 5 to 7 METs covering most club padel:

Body weightRelaxed social game (≈5 METs)Typical club match (≈6 METs)Hard, competitive match (≈7 METs)
60 kg300 kcal360 kcal420 kcal
70 kg350 kcal420 kcal490 kcal
80 kg400 kcal480 kcal560 kcal
90 kg450 kcal540 kcal630 kcal

These are estimates, not measurements. The 7-MET column lines up with the Aspetar figure of about 500 kcal for a 70 kg player.

How hard is padel, really?

The Aspetar review describes padel's metabolic demands as mostly moderate and lower than tennis, with studies reporting intensities of about 50% to 70% of maximum heart rate, oxygen uptake under 45% of maximum, and players covering under 2,000 m in a match. In a study of 8 amateur players over seven one-hour matches (Díaz García and colleagues, 2017), average heart rate was about 130 beats per minute and nearly 90% of playing time was in the aerobic zone. In plain terms: padel is steady aerobic exercise with short bursts, not an all-out sprint session.

What makes you burn more (or less)

  • Body weight. Heavier players use more energy for the same movement.
  • Level and rally length. Better players keep the ball in play longer, so more of the hour is spent moving.
  • Breaks. Time between points, changes of ends and chats all count in the hour but burn little.
  • Effort. Chasing every lob and getting low to every ball costs far more than standing and blocking.
  • Heat. Your heart rate climbs on hot courts, but that doesn't mean proportionally more calories.

Can padel help you lose weight?

It can help, as part of the bigger picture. Two or three hours of padel a week is a meaningful amount of activity: the NHS recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, and lists doubles tennis, padel's closest relative, as an example of moderate activity. But calories burned in exercise are easy to cancel out with a post-match drink and snack, so diet matters more for weight. Padel's biggest advantage is that people enjoy it and keep playing, which is what counts over months.

Fitness trackers estimate calories from heart rate and movement, and padel's stop-start pattern makes those estimates rough. Use them to compare your own sessions, not as exact numbers. If you want padel to do more for your fitness, add strength work twice a week; see is padel good exercise?

Frequently asked questions

How many calories does an hour of padel burn?

Roughly 350 to 550 kcal for most adults. A sports science review uses about 500 kcal per hour for a 70 kg player as a rough guide.

Does padel burn more calories than tennis?

Usually a little less than singles tennis. Research describes padel's demands as mostly moderate and lower than tennis, though a hard padel match can match doubles tennis or more.

Is padel good for weight loss?

It helps as regular, enjoyable exercise, but diet has the bigger effect on weight. Combine padel with strength training and sensible eating.

How many calories does 90 minutes of padel burn?

About one and a half times the hourly figure: roughly 500 to 800 kcal for most adults, depending on weight and intensity.

Are fitness tracker calorie numbers accurate for padel?

They're rough estimates. Padel's stop-start nature makes heart-rate-based estimates less reliable; use them to compare sessions rather than as exact values.
