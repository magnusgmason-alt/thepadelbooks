Every padel shot, explained

Padel has its own vocabulary of shots, built around the walls and the net. Here is what each one is for, with a tactic diagram, and a full guide where we have one.

Bandeja

The controlled, sliced overhead that keeps you at the net when you're lobbed. The most important shot to learn after the basics.

Read the guide →

Víbora

A faster overhead hit on the outside of the ball with side spin, so it skids off the side glass. The bandeja's attacking sibling.

Read the guide →

Smash

The full-power overhead for short, easy lobs. Flat to finish the point, or with topspin so it bounces high off the back glass.

Read the guide →

Lob

A high, deep ball over the net players. The main way to win the net back when you're defending at the back.

Read the guide →

Volley

Hitting the ball before it bounces, mostly at the net. Block the hard ones, punch the easy ones, aim at the feet.

Read the guide →

Chiquita

A soft, low ball at the feet of the net players. Forces them to volley up and gives you time to move forward.

Read the guide →

Bajada

An attacking shot played after the ball comes high off your back glass. Turns defence into attack.

Read the guide →

Off the back glass

Let a deep ball bounce, hit the glass and come back out, then play it. The first wall skill every player needs.

Read the guide →
