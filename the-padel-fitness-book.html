The Padel Fitness Book

Warm-ups, strength, mobility and injury prevention for padel players: self-tests, gym workouts and 8-week plans for every level, including over 40 (2027)

All levels180 pagesBy The Padel Academy2 min read

Play more. Hurt less. Padel is short sprints, deep lunges, quick turns and hundreds of overheads. The Padel Fitness Book shows you how to build a body that keeps up: stronger legs for the lunges, a trunk that turns and protects your back, shoulders and forearms that handle every smash and volley.

It starts with you. Six simple self-tests (balance, squat, shoulder, calf, trunk and grip) show where you're strong and where you're likely to break down. Then come the routines you'll use every week: a padel warm-up, a cool-down and mobility routine, recovery, food and drink, and a plan for match days and tournaments.

The core of the book builds your engine: how to train, then legs and hips, core and rotation, shoulders, forearm, wrist and grip, speed and footwork, conditioning, and a full gym workout done padel-style, with step-by-step instructions for every exercise.

The last part protects your body: the most common padel injuries (elbow, knee, calf, shoulder, back, ankle) and how to come back from them, a chapter on padel after 40, and six ready-made training plans: Starter, Club, Competitive, Over 40, Over 60 and In-season. Every claim is backed by research, with 125 sources listed at the back.

Inside this book
  • 6 self-tests to find your weak links
  • Padel warm-up, cool-down and match-day routines
  • Strength, core, shoulder and grip exercises
  • Speed, footwork and conditioning sessions
  • Common padel injuries and how to come back
  • 8-week plans: Starter, Club, Competitive, Over 40, Over 60, In-season
