How much does padel cost? Court hire, gear and lessons

6 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 30 Oct 2026

How much does padel cost? Usually about £5 to £20 per person for an hour of court time in the UK, and roughly $5 to $35 per person in the US, because four players split one court. In Spain it's often cheaper still. Your first game can cost almost nothing beyond that, since most clubs hire out rackets. This guide gives real 2025 and 2026 prices, with sources, for court hire, kit and lessons, and shows you how to play for less.

How much does padel cost? The short answer

Padel is priced per court, not per player. You book a court for an hour or 90 minutes and split the bill four ways. That's why a court that sounds expensive often works out cheaper than a gym class.

How much is padel per hour?

UK padel court hire prices

The UK is one of the most expensive places to play. Padel Magazine, reporting Playtomic data in August 2025, called the UK the most expensive country to play padel, with an average classic booking of €52 per hour. A later Playtomic comparison, reported in October 2025, put the US higher still (see below), so treat these rankings as snapshots.

In London, Time Out found court prices from £20 to £80 per hour in March 2025. At the cheaper end, Playtime Padel in Kingston charged £20 an hour. The Regent's Park and Hyde Park courts were £40 an hour, and The Padel Hub North London charged £80 at peak times. A newer London guide from Cohabs, published in August 2026, lists courts from £20 an hour off-peak at S3 Padel and Playtime Padel, £24 to £32 an hour at Game4Padel, and around £23 an hour at NTC Padel, with peak central London courts reaching £80 to £100.

US padel court hire prices

US prices range widely by city. Padel Browser's June 2026 guide puts typical court hire at $20 to $60 per hour, but lists The Padel Courts in Los Angeles at $70 per half-hour, while Boar's Head Sports Club in Virginia charged members $32 an hour. Bounce, in July 2026, put a full court for four at $40 to $140. Playtomic data reported in October 2025 made the US the most expensive country in that ranking, with average booking prices of €90 for an indoor court and €60 outdoors.

Spain padel court hire prices

Spain, where padel is a mainstream sport with many courts, is one of the cheapest places to play. The same Playtomic figures gave Spanish averages of €25 indoor and €22 outdoor. In Barcelona, Barcelona Secreta listed court prices at Padel 7 Glòries from €12 to €32 depending on the time and day, though that was in 2024.

How much is padel per person?

Divide the court price by four. Cohabs gives the example of a £24 court split four ways, which comes to £6 each. In the US, Padel Browser estimates about $5 to $15 per person per hour, while Bounce gives a wider $10 to $35. If you play singles on a singles court, you split with one other person instead, so it costs more per head. See our guide to padel singles.

Two other ways to pay per person:

  • Open matches: booking apps let you join a game with strangers at your level and pay only your share.
  • Social sessions: many clubs run mix-ins or social nights for a flat fee. Cohabs puts these at £10 to £12 per person in London.

Cost of starting padel: racket, shoes and balls

You don't need to buy anything for your first game. Most clubs hire rackets, and you can play in trainers. When you're hooked, this is roughly what kit costs:

  • Racket: Cohabs suggests £40 to £50 for an entry-level racket in the UK; Padel Browser puts US beginner rackets at $60 to $130. Our guide to padel racket prices explains what more money buys.
  • Shoes: Padel Browser gives $80 to $140 for padel shoes in the US. For a first session, any court shoe with good side support is fine.
  • Balls: $8 to $12 for a can of three in the US, per Padel Browser. Many clubs expect players to bring a fresh can. Our guide to padel balls explains how long they last.
  • Total: Padel Browser estimates a complete US beginner setup at $100 to $250.

Our padel equipment checklist covers the extras, such as overgrips and a bag, and which ones you can skip.

How much do padel lessons cost?

Lessons usually include the court, and the price per person falls as the group gets bigger. Some real examples:

  • UK: Game4Padel Edinburgh lists £35 an hour off-peak and £45 at peak for one person, falling to £12 per person per hour in a group of four, with the court included.
  • US: Padel Browser puts group clinics at about $20 to $40 per person and private lessons at about $50 to $120 an hour. Bounce gives $75 to $160 for private coaching and suggests confirming whether the price is per player or for the whole lesson.
  • Spain: Cronoshare's January 2026 guide gives €20 to €25 per hour for a private adult lesson, and €3 to €12 per person in a group of four.

Our guide to padel lessons covers whether private or group coaching suits you and how many sessions to book.

Why is padel more expensive in some places?

Court hire reflects how many courts there are and how much they cost to build. In Spain, courts are everywhere and prices are low. In the UK and US, demand has grown faster than supply, and a single new court can cost tens of thousands of pounds or dollars to build, which clubs recover through court fees. Our guide to padel court cost shows the numbers.

How to play padel for less

  1. Book off-peak. Weekday daytime slots are often the cheapest, sometimes much cheaper than evenings.
  2. Always fill the court. Four players means the lowest price per head.
  3. Join open matches and social nights instead of booking a whole court yourself.
  4. Compare venues. In Time Out's 2025 survey, London court prices ranged from £20 to £80 an hour, so the club you choose matters.
  5. Hire before you buy. Play a few times with a club racket before spending on your own.
  6. Share group lessons with friends to split the coach's fee.
  7. Look at memberships if you play weekly. In the US, Padel Browser puts membership plans at $99 and up.

Frequently asked questions

Is padel expensive?

Per person, usually not. A court that costs £24 an hour works out at £6 each for four players. It's pricier in big cities and at peak times, and the UK and US are more expensive than Spain.

How much is padel per hour?

In London, courts cost about £20 to £80 an hour (Time Out, 2025). In the US, typically $20 to $60 an hour, with some city clubs charging over $100. Spanish courts average around €22 to €25 per booking.

Do you pay per person or per court for padel?

Usually per court, split between the players. Open matches, social sessions and group lessons are priced per person.

Can you hire padel rackets?

Yes, most clubs hire rackets, often for around £3 to £5 a session in the UK. It's the cheapest way to try the sport.

How much is a padel lesson?

Examples range from about £35 to £45 an hour for a private lesson at one UK club, $50 to $160 in the US, and €20 to €25 in Spain. Group lessons cost much less per person.
