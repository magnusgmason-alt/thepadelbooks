Padel tactics that win points

Most club matches are won by the pair that stands in the right place, not the one that hits hardest. These are the principles to learn first.

01

Move as a pair

Both at the net or both at the back, never one up and one back.

Read more →
02

Win the net, hold the net

The pair at the net usually wins the point. Lob to get there, bandeja to stay.

Read more →
03

Call every middle ball

Agree who takes the centre before the match: usually the forehand in the middle.

Read more →
04

Left side, right side

One steady builder on the right, one finisher on the left.

Read more →
05

Use the glass, avoid the mesh

Aim deep balls at the glass. The mesh gives unpredictable bounces.

Read more →
06

Lob when you're pushed back

A deep lob over the net pair is the main way to swap defence for attack.
