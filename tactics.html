Padel tactics that win points

Most club matches are won by the pair that stands in the right place, not the one that hits hardest. These are the principles to learn first.

Start here

Where you stand beats how hard you hit

Club padel rewards patience and position. The six principles below decide most matches, from moving as a pair to choosing your side.

  1. 01
    Get to the net

    The pair at the net usually wins the point. The lob is your way forward.

  2. 02
    Move together

    Both up or both back, and agree who takes the middle before you start.

  3. 03
    Be patient

    Keep the ball low and in play. Wait for the short ball before you attack.
