The three rules that cause most arguments

Most padel rules will feel familiar if you've played tennis. These three catch new players out, so learn them first, then look up anything else below.

  1. 01
    The serve

    Bounce the ball behind the service line, hit it underarm at or below waist height, diagonally into the box.

  2. 02
    The walls

    The ball must bounce on the ground before it touches the opponents' glass or fence. After that, the glass is in play.

  3. 03
    The scoring

    Like tennis: 15, 30, 40, game. At deuce, many clubs play a golden point or the newer Star Point.
