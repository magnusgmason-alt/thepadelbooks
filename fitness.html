Warm-up · R1

Easy jog and side shuffle

Heart rate, calves, ankles, hips

How to do it
  1. Jog a few easy lengths of the court.
  2. Turn sideways and shuffle along the baseline, staying low.
  3. Raise the pace a little every length.

Tip Quiet feet are fast feet.

You need None

Dose 2 minutes

Warm-up · R2

Leg swings

Hip flexors, hamstrings, inner thighs

How to do it
  1. Hold the fence with one hand and stand tall.
  2. Swing one leg forwards and back, letting the range grow.
  3. Turn to face the fence and swing it side to side.

Tip The leg moves, the lower back stays still.

You need Fence or glass to hold

Dose 10 each way, each leg

Warm-up · R3

Walking lunge with rotation

Hips, thighs, upper back

How to do it
  1. Step into a long lunge.
  2. Turn your chest towards the front leg.
  3. Come back to the middle and step through to the other side.

Tip Turn from the ribs, not the knee.

You need None

Dose 5 each side

Warm-up · R4

Arm circles and band pull-apart

Shoulders, rotator cuff, upper back

How to do it
  1. Make slow arm circles, small to big, both directions.
  2. Hold a band in front at shoulder height.
  3. Pull it apart until it touches your chest, then return slowly.

Tip Shoulders away from your ears.

You need Light band (optional)

Dose 10 circles each way + 12 pull-aparts

Warm-up · R5

Skips

Calves, Achilles, hip flexors, coordination

How to do it
  1. Skip along the court with a light knee lift.
  2. Stay on the balls of your feet.
  3. Swing the opposite arm with each knee.

Tip Short ground contact, springy rhythm.

You need None

Dose 2 court lengths and back

Warm-up · R6

Mini-band glute activation

Side of the hip, knee control

How to do it
  1. Put a mini-band above your knees and bend them slightly.
  2. Take small side steps, keeping tension in the band.
  3. Finish with a few slow squats, knees pressing out.

Tip Toes forward, hips level.

You need Mini-band

Dose 10 steps each way + 8 squats

Warm-up · R7

Shadow swings

Shoulders, trunk, wrist, timing

How to do it
  1. Swing forehands and backhands without a ball at half speed.
  2. Add volleys, then easy bandejas.
  3. Turn your shoulders fully before every overhead.

Tip Build speed gradually; never snap a cold arm.

You need Racket

Dose About 1 minute

Warm-up · R8

Split steps and sprint starts

Calves, thighs, first step

How to do it
  1. Make a small hop and land on both feet, knees soft.
  2. Push off in any direction for three quick steps.
  3. Walk back and repeat in a new direction.

Tip Land with your weight forward, ready to go.

You need None

Dose 6 to 8 starts

Mobility · M1

Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch

Hip flexors, front of the thigh

How to do it
  1. Kneel on one knee with the other foot in front.
  2. Tuck your pelvis under and squeeze the back glute.
  3. Shift forward until you feel the front of the hip open.

Tip Keep the lower back flat, not arched.

You need Mat or towel

Dose 2 × 30 s each side

Mobility · M2

Standing hamstring stretch

Hamstrings, calf

How to do it
  1. Rest one heel on a low step, leg straight.
  2. Hinge forward from the hips with a long back.
  3. Stop at a gentle pull behind the thigh.

Tip Chest leads, back stays long.

You need Low bench or step

Dose 2 × 30 s each side

Mobility · M3

Wall calf stretch

Calf, soleus, Achilles

How to do it
  1. Hands on the wall, one foot back with the heel down.
  2. Lean in with the back knee straight.
  3. Then bend the back knee slightly to reach the lower calf.

Tip Heel stays on the floor in both versions.

You need Wall

Dose 30 s straight + 30 s bent, each leg

Mobility · M4

Open book

Upper-back rotation, chest

How to do it
  1. Lie on your side, knees bent, arms straight in front.
  2. Lift the top arm and open it across to the other side.
  3. Follow the hand with your eyes, then return.

Tip Breathe out as you open.

You need Mat

Dose 6 to 8 each side

Mobility · M5

Cross-body shoulder stretch

Back of the shoulder

How to do it
  1. Bring one arm across your chest.
  2. Hold it above the elbow with the other hand.
  3. Draw it gently closer until you feel the back of the shoulder.

Tip Keep the stretched shoulder low.

You need None

Dose 2 × 30 s each side

Mobility · M6

Forearm extensor stretch

Back of the forearm, outer elbow

How to do it
  1. Hold one arm out in front, palm down.
  2. Gently bend the wrist down with your other hand.
  3. Hold a mild stretch along the top of the forearm.

Tip Mild only, especially if your elbow is sore.

You need None

Dose 2 × 30 s each arm

Mobility · M7

Figure-4 glute stretch

Glutes, deep hip muscles

How to do it
  1. Lie on your back and cross one ankle over the other knee.
  2. Pull the lower leg towards your chest.
  3. Relax and let the hip open.

Tip On court? Do it seated on a bench and lean forward.

You need Mat (or a bench)

Dose 2 × 30 s each side

Mobility · M8

Doorway chest stretch

Chest, front of the shoulder

How to do it
  1. Place your forearm on a door frame, elbow at shoulder height.
  2. Step through with the same-side foot.
  3. Turn the chest away until you feel a stretch.

Tip Pinching at the front? Lower the elbow.

You need Doorway or glass edge

Dose 2 × 30 s each side

Legs & hips · L1

Goblet squat

Thighs, glutes, inner thighs, trunk

How to do it
  1. Hold a weight at your chest, feet a bit wider than hips.
  2. Sit down between your heels, chest up.
  3. Drive up through the whole foot.

Tip Knees track over the toes.

You need Dumbbell or kettlebell

Starter
2 × 10, light
Club
3 × 8–10
Competitive
3–4 × 6–8, heavier
40+
2–3 × 10–12, slow

Legs & hips · L2

Rear-foot-elevated split squat

Thighs, glutes, hip control

How to do it
  1. Rest the top of your back foot on a bench.
  2. Lower straight down until the front thigh is near level.
  3. Push back up through the front heel.

Tip Think elevator, not escalator.

You need Bench, optional dumbbells

Starter
2 × 6 each leg
Club
3 × 8 each leg
Competitive
3–4 × 6–8 each leg, dumbbells
40+
2 × 8 each leg, hold a support

Legs & hips · L3

Reverse lunge

Thighs, glutes, balance

How to do it
  1. Step one foot back into a lunge.
  2. Lower until both knees are bent.
  3. Push through the front foot to stand.

Tip The front leg does the work.

You need Optional dumbbells

Starter
2 × 8 each leg
Club
3 × 10 each leg
Competitive
3 × 8 each leg, dumbbells
40+
2 × 8 each leg, near a wall

Legs & hips · L4

Lateral lunge

Inner thighs, glutes, thighs

How to do it
  1. Take a wide step to the side.
  2. Sit back into that hip, other leg straight.
  3. Push off to return to the middle.

Tip Hips back, knee follows the toes.

You need Optional kettlebell

Starter
2 × 6 each side
Club
3 × 8 each side
Competitive
3 × 8 each side, with weight
40+
2 × 6–8 each side, partial depth

Legs & hips · L5

Single-leg Romanian deadlift

Hamstrings, glutes, ankle and hip stability

How to do it
  1. Stand on one leg with a soft knee.
  2. Hinge forward as the free leg reaches back.
  3. Return to standing by squeezing the glute.

Tip Head to back heel in one straight line.

You need Optional weight

Starter
2 × 6 each leg
Club
3 × 8 each leg
Competitive
3 × 8 each leg, heavier
40+
2 × 6–8 each leg, fingertips on a wall

Legs & hips · L6

Glute bridge

Glutes, hamstrings

How to do it
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat.
  2. Lift your hips until knees, hips and shoulders line up.
  3. Pause, then lower slowly.

Tip Feel it in your seat, not your lower back.

You need Mat

Starter
2 × 10–12
Club
3 × 12
Competitive
3 × 8 single-leg
40+
2–3 × 12, 2 s hold

Legs & hips · L7

Straight-knee calf raise

Calves, Achilles tendon

How to do it
  1. Stand on a step edge, heels hanging.
  2. Rise as high as you can on your toes.
  3. Lower slowly below the step.

Tip Three seconds down, no bouncing.

You need Step, wall for balance

Starter
2 × 12, both legs
Club
3 × 12, one leg
Competitive
3 × 8–12, one leg + weight
40+
3 × 10–15, slow

Legs & hips · L8

Bent-knee calf raise

Lower calf (soleus), Achilles

How to do it
  1. Bend your knees slightly and keep them bent.
  2. Rise onto your toes.
  3. Lower slowly with the same knee bend.

Tip Only the ankles move.

You need Wall, or bench and dumbbell

Starter
2 × 12, both legs
Club
3 × 12 each leg
Competitive
3 × 8–12 seated, heavy
40+
3 × 12 each leg, slow

Legs & hips · L9

Lateral band walk

Side of the hip

How to do it
  1. Band above the knees, quarter squat.
  2. Step sideways in small steps.
  3. Keep tension on the band the whole time.

Tip Knees press out, toes forward.

You need Mini-band

Starter
2 × 10 steps each way
Club
3 × 12 steps each way
Competitive
3 × 15 steps, band at ankles
40+
2–3 × 10 steps each way

Legs & hips · L10

Step-down

Thighs, glutes, knee control

How to do it
  1. Stand on a low step on one leg.
  2. Slowly lower the other heel towards the floor.
  3. Touch lightly and press back up.

Tip Hips level, knee over the middle toes.

You need Low step (15–20 cm)

Competitive
3 × 10, higher step
40+

Front plank

Abdominals, deep trunk muscles

How to do it
  1. Forearms under shoulders, legs straight.
  2. Lift into one long line from head to heels.
  3. Breathe and hold.

Tip Ribs down, glutes gently squeezed.

Side plank

Obliques, side of the hip

How to do it
  1. Lie on your side on one forearm.
  2. Lift your hips into a straight line.
  3. Hold, then switch sides.

Tip Push the floor away, hips high.

Core & rotation · C3

Dead bug

Deep abdominals, coordination

How to do it
  1. Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent at 90°.
  2. Lower one arm and the opposite leg slowly.
  3. Return and switch sides.

Tip Your lower back stays pressed down.

Core & rotation · C4

Bird dog

Back muscles, glutes, deep abdominals

How to do it
  1. Start on hands and knees.
  2. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back.
  3. Hold, return, switch.

Tip Hips stay level, no twisting.

You need Mat

Core & rotation · C5

Pallof press

Obliques, deep abdominals

How to do it
  1. Stand side-on to a band anchored at chest height.
  2. Hold it at your chest, then press straight out.
  3. Resist the pull for two seconds and bring it back.

Tip Don't let the band turn you.

You need Band or cable

Starter
2 × 8 each side
Club
3 × 10 each side
Competitive
3 × 10, stronger band
40+
2–3 × 8–10 each side

Core & rotation · C6

Half-kneeling band chop

Obliques, trunk, hips

How to do it
  1. Kneel on one knee side-on to a high anchor.
  2. Pull the band diagonally down across your body.
  3. Return slowly against the band.

Tip Hips stay still; the trunk does the turning.

You need Band anchored high

Starter
2 × 8 each side
Club
3 × 10 each side
Competitive
3 × 10, heavier and faster
40+
2 × 8–10 each side

Core & rotation · C7

Rotational medicine-ball throw

Hips, obliques, shoulders

How to do it
  1. Stand side-on to a wall, ball at your back hip.
  2. Turn the hips and throw the ball into the wall.
  3. Catch it and reset before the next throw.

Tip Hips first, arms last.

You need 2–4 kg ball, solid wall

Starter
2 × 5 each side, 2 kg
Club
3 × 6 each side
Competitive
4 × 5 each side, 3–4 kg
40+
2–3 × 5, lighter ball

Core & rotation · C8

Short-lever Copenhagen plank

Inner thighs, obliques

How to do it
  1. Lie on your side with the top knee on a bench.
  2. Lift your hips so your body forms one line.
  3. Hold, keep breathing, then switch.

Tip Start short; this one bites.

You need Bench or sturdy chair

2 × 10–20 s each side

Forearm, wrist & grip · W1

Eccentric wrist extension

Back of the forearm (padel elbow)

How to do it
  1. Rest your forearm on your thigh, palm down, hand over the knee.
  2. Use the other hand to help the weight up.
  3. Lower it slowly on its own for three seconds.

Tip Help it up, control it down.

You need Light dumbbell (1–3 kg)

2 × 10, 1 kg
Club
3 × 12–15
Competitive
3 × 15, a bit heavier
40+
2–3 × 12, slow

Wrist flexion curl

Palm side of the forearm

How to do it

  2. Curl the weight up with the wrist only.
  3. Lower slowly.

Tip The forearm stays down.

You need Light dumbbell

Forearm, wrist & grip · W3

Forearm rotation with a hammer

Forearm rotators, wrist stabilisers

How to do it
  1. Rest your forearm on your thigh, holding a hammer upright.
  2. Turn it slowly to one side.
  3. Bring it back up and turn to the other side.

Tip Hold lower on the handle to make it easier.

Forearm, wrist & grip · W4

Radial and ulnar deviation

Thumb side and little-finger side of the wrist

How to do it
  1. Stand with your arm by your side, holding a hammer.
  2. Tilt the hammer head up using only the wrist.
  3. Lower slowly; then work the other direction.

Tip Small range, full control.

You need Hammer

Competitive
3 × 15 each way
40+

Forearm, wrist & grip · W5

Grip squeeze

Fingers, thumb, small hand muscles

How to do it
  1. Squeeze the ball as hard as is comfortable.
  2. Hold the squeeze.
  3. Let go completely before the next one.

Tip Practise relaxing too; a death grip loads the elbow.

You need Soft ball or hand gripper

Starter
2 × 10, 3 s hold
Club
3 × 10, 5 s hold
Competitive
3 × 8–10, firm gripper
40+
2 × 10, soft ball

Forearm, wrist & grip · W6

Farmer carry

Grip, forearms, shoulders, trunk

How to do it
  1. Pick up a weight in each hand.
  2. Walk tall with short, steady steps.
  3. Put them down with a straight back.

Tip Walk as if balancing a book on your head.

You need Two dumbbells or kettlebells

Starter
3 × 20 m
Club
3–4 × 30 m
Competitive
4 × 30–40 m, heavy
40+
3 × 20 m, moderate
