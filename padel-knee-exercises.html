Padel knee exercises: a 6-week plan for stronger knees

4 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 17 Nov 2026

Strong thighs, hips and calves are what let your knees cope with lunges, braking and split steps. This 6-week plan uses eight exercises, takes about 25 minutes twice a week, and builds from easy to padel-ready. You can add any of them to a free personal PDF in our workout builder.

Not medical advice

This guide explains common patterns in plain language. It can't diagnose you. If the pain is severe, came from a fall or twist, or isn't settling, get it assessed.

Why strength beats stretching for padel knees

The evidence for strength work is strong. A 2014 meta-analysis of injury-prevention trials (Lauersen and colleagues, BJSM) found that strength training reduced sports injuries to less than a third, while stretching showed no clear protective effect. For front-of-knee pain, the 2016 international consensus (Crossley and colleagues) recommends combining hip and knee exercises rather than knee exercises alone. So the plan below trains the thigh, the hip and the calf together.

The eight exercises

ExerciseWhy padel players need it
Goblet squatStrength for getting low on volleys and digging balls out of the glass
Rear-foot-elevated split squatSingle-leg strength for lunges, one leg at a time
Reverse lungeControlled lunging that is easier to control than a forward lunge
Step-downTeaches the knee to track over the toes as you lower, the key to braking
Lateral band walkSide-of-hip strength that stops the knee caving in
Glute bridgeHip extensors that share the load with your thighs
Lateral lungeSideways strength for wide balls
Calf raiseShock absorption from the ankle up

The 6-week plan

Two sessions a week on days you don't play hard, at least one rest day apart. Rest about 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

1–2: learnGoblet squat 2 × 10 · Step-down 2 × 8 each leg · Band walk 2 × 10 steps each way · Bridge 2 × 12Reverse lunge 2 × 8 each leg · Lateral lunge 2 × 6 each side · Calf raise 2 × 12 · Bridge 2 × 12
3–4: buildGoblet squat 3 × 8–10 · Split squat 3 × 8 each leg · Band walk 3 × 12 · Calf raise 3 × 12Reverse lunge 3 × 10 each leg · Step-down 3 × 10 each leg · Lateral lunge 3 × 8 each side · Bridge 3 × 12
5–6: padel-readyGoblet squat 3–4 × 6–8, heavier · Split squat 3 × 8 each leg with dumbbells · Single-leg calf raise 3 × 10Reverse lunge 3 × 8 each leg with dumbbells · Step-down 3 × 10, higher step · Lateral lunge 3 × 8 with weight · Single-leg bridge 3 × 8

Over 40, or coming back from knee pain? Stay in weeks 1 to 2 for longer, use a support for balance, and only add load when every rep feels controlled. Mild effort or discomfort is fine; sharp pain is a signal to reduce the range or the weight.

Three technique rules that protect your knee

  1. Knee over the middle toes. On squats, lunges and step-downs, the knee travels in line with the foot rather than caving inwards.
  2. Slow on the way down. Two to three seconds lowering builds the braking strength padel needs.
  3. Use the hips. Sit back slightly into every squat and lunge so the glutes share the work.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best knee exercises for padel?

Squats, split squats, reverse lunges, step-downs and lateral lunges for the thighs, plus band walks and bridges for the hips and calf raises for shock absorption.

How often should I do knee exercises for padel?

Twice a week is enough for most club players, on days you aren't playing hard, with at least a day in between.

Can knee exercises prevent padel injuries?

Strength training is one of the best-supported ways to reduce sports injuries. A 2014 meta-analysis found it reduced injuries to less than a third.

Are lunges bad for your knees?

No. Done with control and the knee tracking over the toes, lunges build exactly the strength padel needs. Reverse lunges are easier to control, so they make a good starting point.

Can I do these exercises with knee pain?

Often yes, with smaller ranges and lighter loads. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain, and get a sore knee assessed if it isn't improving.
