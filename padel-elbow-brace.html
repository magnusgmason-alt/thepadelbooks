Elbow brace for padel: does a tennis elbow strap work?

4 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 17 Nov 2026

Walk around any padel club and you'll see forearm straps everywhere. Do they work? The honest answer: they can make playing more comfortable in the short term, the evidence is modest, and they work best alongside strengthening, not instead of it. Here's what the research says and how to use one sensibly.

Not medical advice

This guide explains common patterns in plain language. It can't diagnose you. If the pain is severe, came from a fall or twist, or isn't settling, get it assessed.

What a tennis elbow strap does

A counterforce strap is a band worn around the upper forearm, just below the elbow. The idea is that it spreads the pull of the forearm muscles over a wider area, so less load reaches the sore tendon on the outside of the elbow. Physiopedia's tennis elbow toolkit cites a combined cadaver and clinical study that measured a 13 to 15% reduction in force at the tendon origin with a counterforce brace.

What the evidence says

  • Short-term pain: there is weak evidence that a brace, sleeve or splint reduces immediate pain, and that a counterforce brace may reduce pain over the first few weeks.
  • With exercise: a placebo-controlled trial found that adding a counterforce brace to exercise-based rehab improved pain and function in the short and long term.
  • Taping: a few trials show taping can give immediate pain relief, but it hasn't consistently improved strength.
  • NHS advice: the NHS lists trying a forearm strap, or a wrist or elbow brace from a pharmacy, among its self-care steps for tennis elbow.

Bottom line: a strap is a reasonable, cheap thing to try for comfort. It's not a cure.

How to wear a strap for padel

  • Place it on the upper forearm, a little below the sore spot, following the product's instructions.
  • Snug, not tight: you should be able to grip comfortably and your fingers shouldn't tingle or change colour.
  • Wear it for playing and gripping tasks, not all day and night.
  • Take it off if it increases pain, numbness or swelling.

Use it as a bridge, not a crutch

The strap buys you comfort while the real fixes work: less load, a relaxed grip, better technique and strength. Pair it with our 6-week padel elbow plan and the decision guide can I play padel with tennis elbow?. If the elbow still hurts after two weeks of self-care, see a GP or physio.

Frequently asked questions

Does a tennis elbow strap work for padel?

It can reduce pain in the short term for some players. The evidence is modest, and it works best combined with exercise and reduced load.

Where should I wear a tennis elbow strap?

On the upper forearm, a little below the painful spot on the outside of the elbow, snug but not tight. Follow the product's instructions.

Should I wear an elbow brace all day?

No. Wear it for padel and gripping tasks. Remove it if it causes numbness, tingling or more pain.

Is a sleeve or a strap better for padel elbow?

A counterforce strap targets the tendon more directly; a sleeve mainly offers warmth and comfort. Many padel players prefer a strap.

Can a brace prevent tennis elbow?

There's little evidence for that. Gradual increases in playing time, a relaxed grip, good technique and forearm strength are better prevention.
