Padel drills for 4 players

7 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 23 Oct 2026

The best padel drills for 4 players turn a normal doubles booking into focused practice: two pairs, one theme and a score. Below are eight four-player drills that build net play, lobs, transitions and teamwork, plus a section of padel drills for 3 players for the days when someone drops out.

Getting the most from padel drills for 4 players

With four players the risk is that the session drifts into a casual match. Drills keep it focused. A few rules help:

  • One theme per session. For example net play, defence, or the transition from back to front.
  • Rotate partners. Swap pairs every drill so the stronger players don't always play together.
  • Use conditioned points. Normal points with one added rule teach far more than plain matchplay, because the rule forces the habit you are training.
  • Finish with a match. Ten minutes of normal points tests whether the drill has stuck.

Net drills for four

1. Volley war

Players: 4 · Time: 10 minutes

Setup: All four at the net, about two metres back. Start with straight pairs: one pair facing each other in each lengthways half of the court.

How it works: Each pair volleys with each other at the same time, staying in its own half. Keep the ball low, just over the net, aiming at your opponent's feet.

Goal: First pair to complete a 15-volley rally wins the round. Play five rounds.

Progression: Switch to diagonal pairs, at a controlled pace, so the two balls cross in the middle. Then use a single ball for all four players and play points from the net. Lobs are banned, so the rally is all volleys.

2. Defend the net

Players: 4 · Time: 12 minutes

Setup: One pair at the net, one pair at the back.

How it works: The back pair starts every point with a feed and may only lob. The net pair must answer every lob with a bandeja or víbora and stay forward. When a lob is too good and the net pair has to run back, the back pair moves up and the roles reverse.

Goal: The net pair scores a point for every rally they win without losing the net. Swap roles after ten points.

Progression: Allow the back pair to play chiquitas as well as lobs. The net pair now has to cover low balls and high ones.

Transition drills

3. Lob and take the net

Players: 4 · Time: 12 minutes

Setup: Pairs start in opposite positions: one at the net, one at the back.

How it works: Play normal points, but the back pair earns a bonus point every time it lobs over the net pair and both back players reach the net before the next ball. Move together, as a pair. The Padel School's warning is the key to this drill: when your lob is not good, don't come to the net.

Goal: First pair to fifteen (normal points plus bonuses).

Progression: The bonus only counts if both players get past the service line and into proper net position, not stuck in mid-court.

4. Serve and stay

Players: 4 · Time: 10 minutes

Setup: A normal doubles start: server and partner on one side, receiving pair on the other.

How it works: The server must follow the serve to the net. The receiving pair aims to win the net back with the return, often a lob or a low ball at the server's feet.

Goal: Play service games with normal scoring. The serving pair counts how many points it wins while still at the net.

Progression: Second serves only. With less margin on the serve, the first volley matters more.

Teamwork drills

5. Middle madness

Players: 4 · Time: 10 minutes

Setup: Pairs on each side of the net, both at the back or both at the net.

How it works: Every shot must go through the middle third of the court, between imaginary lines about 3.3 metres in from each side wall. Players must call "mine", "yours" or "go" on every ball.

Goal: Normal points. An uncalled ball, or both players going for it, loses the point immediately.

Progression: Open the court back up but award two points for any winner hit through the middle. See our doubles positioning guide for who should take it.

6. Rope pairs

Players: 4 · Time: 10 minutes

Setup: Normal doubles points, full court.

How it works: Play points while consciously staying level with your partner, as though tied together with a short rope. When one moves forward, both move forward; when one is lobbed, both drop back.

Goal: A pair loses a point if, at the moment an opponent hits, one partner is at the net and the other is at the back.

Progression: Add a second condition: the pair also loses the point if both players end up on the same side of the centre line when an opponent hits.

Match-pressure games

7. Yellow card, red card

Players: 4 · Time: 15 minutes

Setup: The full court, normal doubles.

How it works: Normal games. A pair's first mistake in a game is a yellow card, whichever partner makes it; the second is a red card and loses the game. The LTA uses this game to build consistency.

Goal: Most games in fifteen minutes.

Progression: Our own variation: a mistake made from the net position is an instant red card. It sharpens volleys and overheads.

8. King of the court

Players: 4 (works with 5 or 6) · Time: 15 minutes

Setup: One side of the net is the "king" side. The challengers start on the other side.

How it works: The challengers start each point with a feed. If the kings win, they stay and score a point. If the challengers win, they move to the king side. With six players, the losing challengers swap out for the waiting pair. With five, the losing challenger who has played longest sits out and the waiting player steps in.

Goal: First pair to seven points on the king side.

Progression: The kings must win two points in a row to score.

Padel drills for 3 players

Three players is a great number for drilling: one feeder works two hitters, or two players gang up on one. Rotate every five to ten minutes so everyone gets every role.

9. Two against one at the net

Players: 3 · Time: 12 minutes

Setup: A pair at the net on one side; the single player at the back on the other, covering only one half of the court (cross-court).

How it works: The single player feeds and then defends in their half, using lobs, low balls and the glass. The pair can only hit into that half.

Goal: The single player scores one point for every five balls kept in play, and three for winning a rally. Rotate after ten feeds.

Progression: The single player may only lob, which turns it into overhead practice for the pair.

10. Feeder and pair

Players: 3 · Time: 10 minutes

Setup: A feeder with a basket on one side; a pair on the other.

How it works: The feeder plays three-ball sequences: a low ball to the feet, a ball down the middle, then a lob. The pair volleys, calls the middle and plays a bandeja, then resets.

Goal: Count sequences where all three balls go back in. Eight out of ten is a strong score.

Progression: After the third ball, the feeder plays the point out against the pair using one half of the court.

Balancing mixed levels

In a four with uneven levels, mix the pairs; if one pair is still clearly weaker, give it a head start (for example 15–0 every game). Everyone stays competitive.

A 90-minute four-player plan

MinutesActivity
0–15Warm-up: 5 minutes of mini-rallies, then volley war (10)
15–40Defend the net, then lob and take the net (12 + 12, plus changeover)
40–60Middle madness, then rope pairs (10 + 10)
60–75Yellow card, red card
75–90Normal match, rotating partners

Frequently asked questions

What are good padel drills for 4 players?

Drills that use conditioned points: defend the net, lob and take the net, and yellow card, red card. They feel like a match but train one habit at a time.

What padel drills work for 3 players?

Two-against-one drills and feeder drills. One player defends a half-court against a pair at the net, or a feeder plays set sequences to two hitters. Rotate roles regularly.

How do you keep four players busy in drills?

Choose drills where all four hit the ball, like volley war or conditioned points. Avoid single-feeder drills with a queue when you have four.

Should we drill or play matches?

Both. A good split is two-thirds drills, one-third match, with the match at the end so you can test what you practised.
