The Padel Drill Book: Intermediate & Advanced

80 drills for levels 3 to 6: reads, patterns and pressure (2027)

Level 3 → 6165 pagesBy The Padel Academy2 min read

You already know the shots. This book trains the decisions. Past level 3, points are rarely lost because a shot is missing. They are lost because the wrong shot was chosen, a beat too late, or because a pair moved in two directions at once. Every one of these 80 drills puts a real read in front of you, from "bajada or lob?" to "go forward or stay back?", so the right answer becomes automatic in a match.

The drills are grouped by the moment of the point they train: glass mastery, owning the net, the overhead arsenal (bandeja, víbora, rulo, gancho, por tres and por cuatro), transitions, defence under fire, serve, return and the first four shots, pair patterns and the Australian formation, and twelve pressure games that make every point cost something.

Each drill has its own page with a court diagram, the set-up, four steps, the decision it trains, the fault to watch for, how to level it up or down, and a pass mark. Sixteen shot and tactic reference diagrams sit at the start of the chapters they belong to.

To finish, sixteen ready-made sessions, chapter checklists, three 8-week plans (level 3 to 4, 4 to 5 and 5 to 6), session logs, blank courts and a glossary. A companion to The Padel Drill Book and The Padel Playbook: Intermediate & Advanced.
