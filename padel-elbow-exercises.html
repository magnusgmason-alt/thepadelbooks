Padel elbow exercises: a 6-week plan for tennis elbow

4 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 17 Nov 2026

Exercise is the best-studied treatment for tennis elbow, and the core of it is simple: load the forearm tendons slowly and progressively so they can cope with padel again. This 6-week plan uses six exercises and about 15 minutes a session. Start gently, progress only when it feels easy, and keep playing at a level your elbow tolerates.

Not medical advice

This guide explains common patterns in plain language. It can't diagnose you. If the pain is severe, came from a fall or twist, or isn't settling, get it assessed.

What the research supports

Two findings shape this plan. First, slow lowering (eccentric) work for the wrist extensors helps: in a randomised trial of 21 people with chronic tennis elbow (Tyler and colleagues, 2010), adding an eccentric wrist-extensor exercise to standard physiotherapy produced much bigger improvements in pain, function and strength than standard physiotherapy alone. Second, it works best as part of a wider programme: a systematic review (Cullinane and colleagues, 2014) supported eccentric exercise within multimodal therapy rather than on its own, and current expert opinion recommends strengthening the whole arm, shoulder and shoulder blade too.

The six exercises

ExerciseWhy
Eccentric wrist extensionThe key exercise: slow lowering for the tendons on the outside of the elbow
Wrist flexion curlBalances the other side of the forearm
Forearm rotation with a hammerControl for the twisting forces of volleys and overheads
Grip squeezeGrip strength, and practice at fully relaxing the hand
Band external rotationShoulder strength so the forearm isn't doing the shoulder's job
Band pull-apartUpper back and shoulder-blade control

The 6-week plan

Three sessions a week, on non-consecutive days. Use a light dumbbell or a water bottle; a hammer works for rotations.

WeeksForearmShoulder
1–2Eccentric wrist extension 2 × 10, about 1 kg, 3 s down · Wrist curl 2 × 12 · Grip squeeze 2 × 10, 3 s holdBand external rotation 2 × 12 · Band pull-apart 2 × 12
3–4Eccentric wrist extension 3 × 12–15 · Wrist curl 3 × 15 · Hammer rotation 2 × 10 each way · Grip squeeze 3 × 10, 5 s holdExternal rotation 3 × 15 · Pull-apart 3 × 15
5–6Eccentric wrist extension 3 × 15, slightly heavier · Wrist curl 3 × 15 · Hammer rotation 3 × 10, longer lever · Firm gripper 3 × 8–10External rotation 3 × 15, firmer band · Pull-apart 3 × 15

How much pain is OK? Mild discomfort during the exercises is common with tendon problems. A widely used rule in tendon rehabilitation is that pain up to about 5 out of 10 during exercise is acceptable if it settles back to normal by the next morning. Sharp pain, or a worse elbow the next day, means reduce the weight or the range.

How to do the key exercise properly

  1. Sit with your forearm on your thigh or a table, palm down, hand hanging over the edge.
  2. Use your other hand to help lift the weight up.
  3. Let go and lower it slowly on its own, over about three seconds.
  4. Help it back up and repeat. Up with help, down on your own.

Exercises alone won't fix it if the load stays the same

Keep padel at a level the elbow tolerates while you strengthen: shorter sessions, a relaxed grip, volleys from the shoulder rather than the wrist. If it hasn't improved after six weeks of home treatment, the NHS says physiotherapy may help. Read can I play padel with tennis elbow? for how to manage court time.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best exercise for padel elbow?

The eccentric wrist extension: help the weight up with your other hand, then lower it slowly on its own. It's best done as part of a wider forearm and shoulder programme.

How long do tennis elbow exercises take to work?

Tendons adapt slowly, so expect weeks rather than days. Most programmes run for at least 6 to 12 weeks.

Should tennis elbow exercises hurt?

Mild discomfort is common. Pain up to a moderate level that settles by the next morning is generally acceptable; sharp pain or a worse elbow the next day is not.

Can I use a resistance bar (FlexBar) for padel elbow?

Yes, the twisting rubber-bar exercise is a form of eccentric wrist-extension training and was used in the Tyler 2010 trial. A dumbbell works too.

Do I need to strengthen my shoulder for tennis elbow?

Current expert opinion recommends it. Shoulder and shoulder-blade strength helps the whole arm share the load.
