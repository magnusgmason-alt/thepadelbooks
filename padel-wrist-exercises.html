Padel wrist exercises: a 6-week plan for stronger wrists

4 min readBy The Padel AcademyUpdated 17 Nov 2026

Your wrist takes a small hit on every volley, every bandeja and every ball you dig out of the glass. Strong forearm and grip muscles make that load easier to absorb. This plan uses six exercises, needs only a light dumbbell, a hammer and a soft ball, and fits into 15 minutes twice a week.

Not medical advice

This guide explains common patterns in plain language. It can't diagnose you. If the pain is severe, came from a fall or twist, or isn't settling, get it assessed.

Why padel wrists need strength

Most padel wrist problems build up slowly. In a 2025 study of 364 amateur players in Chile (Belmar-Arriagada and colleagues), 81.8% of wrist and hand injuries came on gradually rather than from a single incident, which points to repeated load. Strength training is the best-supported way to make tissues tolerate load: a 2014 meta-analysis of injury-prevention trials (Lauersen and colleagues) found it reduced sports injuries to less than a third.

The six exercises

ExerciseWhat it trains
Eccentric wrist extensionBack of the forearm; the muscles that also cause padel elbow
Wrist flexion curlPalm side of the forearm
Forearm rotation with a hammerControl of the twisting forces on off-centre hits
Radial and ulnar deviationThe thumb side and little-finger side of the wrist
Grip squeezeGrip strength, and learning to relax the hand
Farmer carryGrip endurance plus shoulder and trunk stability

The 6-week plan

Two sessions a week, on days you aren't playing hard.

WeeksSession
1–2Wrist curl 2 × 12, 1–2 kg · Eccentric extension 2 × 10, 1 kg · Hammer rotation 2 × 8 each way · Side-to-side tilts 2 × 10 each way · Grip squeeze 2 × 10, 3 s hold
3–4Wrist curl 3 × 15 · Eccentric extension 3 × 12–15 · Hammer rotation 3 × 10 · Tilts 3 × 12 · Grip squeeze 3 × 10, 5 s hold · Farmer carry 3 × 20 m
5–6Wrist curl 3 × 15–20, heavier · Eccentric extension 3 × 15 · Hammer rotation 3 × 12, hold lower on the handle · Tilts 3 × 15 · Firm gripper 3 × 8–10 · Farmer carry 3–4 × 30 m

Over 40, or the wrist is already a bit sore? Stay in weeks 1 to 2 longer, use lighter weights and smaller ranges. Mild effort is fine; sharp pain is not.

Three rules for wrist exercises

  1. Slow beats heavy. Two to three seconds on the way down.
  2. Only the wrist moves. Rest the forearm on your thigh or a table so the elbow can't help.
  3. Practise letting go. After every grip squeeze, relax the hand completely. On court, a relaxed grip between shots protects both wrist and elbow.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best wrist exercises for padel?

Wrist curls, eccentric wrist extension, forearm rotation with a hammer, side-to-side wrist tilts, grip squeezes and farmer carries.

How often should I do wrist exercises for padel?

Twice a week is enough for most players, on days you aren't playing hard.

Can wrist exercises prevent padel injuries?

Strength training is one of the best-supported ways to reduce sports injuries overall. Combined with a relaxed grip and good technique, it makes wrist overload less likely.

What weight should I use?

Start with 1 to 2 kg, or a water bottle, and increase only when every rep feels controlled.

Can I do these with a sore wrist?

Often yes, with lighter loads and smaller ranges. Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain, and get a wrist checked if it isn't improving.
