## Shared padel injury data
- Elbow most common site, then knee, shoulder, lower back; 8 studies, 2,022 players → Dahmen 2023 abstract (fetched via DOAJ).
- Knee ~10–23%, shoulder ~7–34%, lower back ~9–28% of injuries; tennis leg 22.1% by region in one study → Dahmen 2023 (site-verified, padel-injuries table).
- Patellar tendinopathy 2–4× more likely in men; medial meniscus/MCL injuries common (valgus); shoulder overuse from smash/bandeja, supraspinatus most affected; trunk loaded by smash/kick smash, jumping, rotation/extension, trunk problems more frequent than tennis; tennis leg highest in poorly conditioned men >40; muscle strains more common >35; inadequate warm-up a risk factor; recreational players hit backhands with more flexed wrist → Eirale & de Gasperis 2023, Aspetar (fetched).
- 645 French players, 52% injured, calf–Achilles complex 18.8% most affected, elbow 18.5%, lower back most frequent pain, age >40 OR 4.09 → Lecler et al. 2025, J Traumatol Sport (fetched, em-consulte).
- Chile: 364 players, 53.8% injured in 6 months, no age/sex/level difference, 81.8% of wrist–hand injuries gradual onset → Belmar-Arriagada et al. 2025 (fetched, Cumbria repository).
- Herringbone soles linked to muscle/tendon/ligament injuries; sand courts more muscle/ligament injuries; more weekly match hours → more injury; cross-sectional, n=216 → Rocamora-López & Mateo-Orcajada 2025 (fetched, MDPI).
- Recreational players 2–3 sessions/week of 60–120 min; HR 140–160 bpm (70–80% max); 40–50% VO2max; rallies 10–15 s, work:rest ~1:1.13–1:1.41; 2,500–3,500 m per match; no padel intervention trials, all cross-sectional → Sánchez-Alcaraz & Courel-Ibáñez 2022, IJERPH (fetched).

## 1 padel-knee-pain
- Symptom → cause (tendonitis below kneecap from running/jumping; twisting sprains; pop/instability = ligament/meniscus); self-care ice ≤20 min every 2–3 h; GP if not improving in a few weeks; urgent list; "do not self-diagnose" → NHS knee pain (fetched).
- Patellar tendinitis location, pain at start of activity/after hard workout, risk factors (running/jumping, sharp increase, tight quads/hamstrings, imbalance) → Mayo Clinic (fetched).
- Meniscus: forceful twist/rotate, pivoting sports incl. tennis, age, pop/swelling/locking/giving way, symptoms may be delayed → Mayo Clinic torn meniscus (fetched).
- Anterior knee pain description, stairs/chair, tight/weak hip & knee muscles, keep activity at a non-painful level → UHL NHS leaflet (fetched).
- Combine hip + knee exercises in preference to knee alone; foot orthoses short-term; taping unclear; no separate brace recommendation → Crossley et al. 2016 (fetched).

## 2 padel-wrist-pain
- Wrist causes list (heavier racket, unsuitable grip size, rapid volume increase); support can't treat cause; build up gradually → Rehaboteket, physio-reviewed (fetched).
- TFCC definition, pinky side, racket sports, clicking, grip weakness, non-surgical first, brace early, rehab grip/rotation/control → Princeton Medicine (fetched).
- De Quervain's/thumb symptoms, self-care (ice, splint, avoid heat first 2–3 days), GP after 2 weeks, urgent list → NHS wrist pain (fetched).
- Don't stop completely, reduce activity; A&E after fall with pain/difficulty moving → Leicestershire Partnership NHS leaflet (fetched).
- Bandeja coaching: turn side-on, swing across body, take pace off (LTA Padel, fetched); turn early, contact in front, move feet (The Padel School, fetched).

## 3 padel-shoulder-pain
- Rotator cuff pain when load exceeds capacity; up to 70% of shoulder pain; symptoms; ≥12 weeks, up to 24, may be no change first 6 weeks; exercise best treatment; pain during exercise OK if it settles → LPT NHS leaflet (fetched).
- Mild shoulder pain 4–6 weeks; keep moving; GP at 2 weeks; ED red flags → nidirect (fetched).
- Handball trial: 660 players, 3×/week in warm-up, IR range, ER strength, scapular strength, thoracic mobility, kinetic chain; 28% lower risk (OR 0.72) → Andersson 2017 BJSM (fetched abstract).
- Exercise selection in the routine is our own, built on those components (not a copy of the trial programme) — stated as such in text.

## 4 padel-lower-back-pain
- Keep active; flare-ups settle within 6 weeks, most improvement first 2 weeks; GP if persistent/unchanging/worse at night; cauda equina red flags → NHS inform (fetched).
- Strength training cut injuries to < one-third, stretching no benefit → Lauersen 2014 (site-verified).

## 5 padel-calf-strain
- Tennis leg: medial gastrocnemius myotendinous tear, knee extension + forced dorsiflexion, middle age, sharp pain/snap/swelling/gap, conservative care → Radiopaedia (fetched).
- Achilles rupture symptoms (kicked feeling, pop, can't push off/tiptoe), peak age 30–40, men up to 5×, tennis, steroid injections and fluoroquinolones, prevention (strengthen/stretch calves, raise intensity slowly) → Mayo Clinic via Mayo Clinic Care Network library (fetched, kuh.ku.edu.tr copy).
- Tendinopathy 2–6 cm above insertion, risk factors; rupture can be missed, delayed treatment worse, stop weight-bearing → Patient.info professional reference (fetched).
- PRICE first 2–3 days, ice ≤20 min every 2–3 h, avoid heat/alcohol/massage, ~2 weeks, avoid strenuous exercise up to 8 weeks, A&E signs, don't drive → NHS 111 Wales (fetched).
- Calf raise stages = our general progression, labelled as starting points.

## 6 padel-over-40
- Load management particularly relevant for older adults; rest/lighter sessions; build frequency gradually → Padel New Zealand (fetched).
- Strength 2 days/week, 150 min moderate → NHS adults 19–64 (site-verified).
- Racket advice (lighter, low balance, softer) presented as common advice with little research, consistent with /padel-elbow.

## 7 padel-for-seniors
- 65+: active daily, strength/balance/flexibility 2 days, 150 min moderate or 75 vigorous; balance exercises help if worried about falling → NHS older adults (fetched).
- Speak to doctor if inactive/medical conditions → Guy's and St Thomas' NHS FT (fetched).
- Low impact, short learning curve, no high fitness needed, isolation/loneliness, Soul Timers, Parkinson's sessions, court finder, LTA comps 35+ to 80+, rankings 40+/50+/60+, Legends Padel 60+ → LTA Padel (fetched).
- FIP senior categories 40+ to 60+, Senior World Cup Buenos Aires 14–19 Sept 2026 → FIP (fetched).
- Padel studies skew young; none in older adults; no social outcomes measured → Lauxtermann & Stubbs 2025 (fetched).
- Chest pain: stop, sit, rest; call 999 if it doesn't settle → Moving Medicine / FSEM (fetched; source wording is for people with angina, so text kept general).
- "Walking padel" sessions exist (Playtomic listings seen in search only; format not described in text).

## 8 padel-cool-down-stretches
- 5 min easy, then 5–10 min static, 20–30 s holds, no bouncing; muscle list → Padel New Zealand (fetched).
- Stretching → no clinically important DOMS reduction; post-exercise ≈1 point on 100-point scale → Herbert et al. 2011 Cochrane (conclusion and numbers read via PainScience summary; Cochrane page confirmed).
- Active cool-down doesn't substantially reduce DOMS or prevent injury → Van Hooren & Peake 2018 (fetched).
- Static stretching small–moderate acute performance drop, mostly ≥60 s holds → Behm 2016 (site-verified).

## 9 what-to-eat-before-padel
- Last meal 2–4 h; snack in the hour before; carb-rich, low fibre, low fat/protein, familiar; food examples; sip fluid over hours → Sports Dietitians Australia (fetched).
- Caffeine 2–3 mg/kg (~200 mg), plateau ~3 mg/kg; side effects incl. impaired fine motor control; ≤400 mg/day, ≤200 mg at once; half-life ~5 h; brewed coffee ~80 mg/250 ml; large servings >500 mg; pregnancy exception → AIS caffeine fact sheet (fetched).
- Electrolytes useful for high sweat rates/long/salty sweat/heat; 0.5–0.7 g sodium per litre; rehydrate 1.2–1.5× deficit within 2–4 h with sodium → AIS electrolytes fact sheet (fetched).
- Refuel immediately when recovery is short; delaying feeding reduces glycogen storage; 30–60 g/h when play >2 h; sodium losses 0.5–1.8 g, depletion linked to heat cramps → Ranchordas et al. 2013 (fetched).
- 20–40 g protein (0.25–0.40 g/kg) per dose; 0–2 h post-exercise → Kerksick et al. 2017 ISSN (fetched).
- Drink regularly regardless of thirst; ~1–2.5 L/h; avoid 11am–3pm; stop and rest in shade if dizzy → LTA heat guidance (fetched).

## 10 padel-health-benefits
- Racket sports: 80,306 adults, mean age 52, ~9-year follow-up, 47% lower all-cause and 56% lower CVD mortality, observational, small number of deaths, self-report → BMJ press release on Oja 2017 (fetched).
- 8 padel mental-health studies, n=538, performance focus, no social outcomes, skew young/male → Lauxtermann & Stubbs 2025 (fetched).
- Social/adherence claims and player testimony → LTA Padel health article (fetched; its unsourced 70–85% HR figure and over-55 inactivity stats NOT used).
- Cutting calories more effective than exercise for weight loss; combination helps; active people maintain loss → Mayo Clinic Q&A via Mayo Clinic Care Network (fetched).
