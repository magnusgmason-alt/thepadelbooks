# 80 drill concepts (levels 3–6). Chapter letters = drill id prefix.

## 01 Glass Mastery (G, 10)
G1 Call the drop: read the rebound and call where the ball will come down before hitting (3–5)
G2 Glass to deep cross-court lob against a closing net pair, on a time limit (3–5)
G3 Glass to chiquita at the incoming volleyer's feet (3–5)
G4 Corner exit, pick the target: after side+back glass choose lob, chiquita or drive by the feeder's position (4–6)
G5 Bajada or lob, the read: feeder sends deep lobs of varying height; attack only the high rebound (4–6)
G6 Pinned in the corner: contra-pared escape when the ball has passed you (4–6)
G7 Low exit through the middle seam off the back glass (3–5)
G8 The ball that dies: sliced feeds that sit low after the glass (3–5)
G9 Four-wall patience rally: 2 v 2 cooperative rally where every ball must use a wall, then go live (3–5)
G10 Glass then up: when the exit wins time, the pair moves forward together (4–6)

## 02 Owning the Net (N, 10)
N1 Volley clock: feeder calls target 1–4 as the ball is struck (3–5)
N2 Two-speed volley: block deep vs punch to the feet depending on the incoming height (3–5)
N3 Dig and step in: low ball at the feet, lift it deep, then close (3–5)
N4 Angle finish into the side glass on the high ball (4–6)
N5 Through the seam: volley between the two opponents (3–5)
N6 Two-feeder reflex: two feeders, one volleyer, fast alternating balls (4–6)
N7 Body ball defence at the net: ball at the chest/hip (4–6)
N8 Six deep, then attack: patience chain before the put-away (3–5)
N9 Slide as a wall: net pair shifts sideways with the ball (3–5)
N10 Poach and cover: B crosses to intercept, A covers the vacated side (5–6)

## 03 The Overhead Arsenal (O, 10)
O1 Bandeja lanes: feeder calls left / middle / right lane while the lob is in the air (3–5)
O2 Bandeja under a deep lob: retreat footwork then forward recovery (3–5)
O3 Víbora to the corner pocket (4–6)
O4 The rulo: topspin overhead that kicks off the back glass (5–6)
O5 The gancho over the backhand side (5–6)
O6 X3 or bandeja: decide from the ball and position (5–6)
O7 X4 and the follow: what to do if it comes back (5–6)
O8 Smash, recover, reset to net position (4–6)
O9 Overhead on call: feeder calls bandeja / víbora / smash late (4–6)
O10 Overhead, then cover the counter-lob as a pair (4–6)

## 04 Transitions (T, 10)
T1 Earn the net with the lob: only move up after a lob that really passes them (3–5)
T2 Chiquita and close together (3–5)
T3 Losing the net well: retreat together after a good lob against you (3–5)
T4 Transition ladder: back – middle – net, one ball per zone (3–5)
T5 The second-ball attack after a weak reply (4–6)
T6 Escape the middle: caught at the service line, get out fast (3–5)
T7 Defence to attack in three balls (4–6)
T8 Drive and follow at the right moment (4–6)
T9 Counter-chiquita: net pair answers a low ball without losing the net (4–6)
T10 Net is worth double: 2 v 2 scoring game rewarding transitions (3–6)

## 05 Defence Under Fire (D, 10)
D1 Smash defence: let it reach the glass, then lob (4–6)
D2 Counter-lob from the back glass (3–5)
D3 Defending the víbora corner ball (4–6)
D4 Body shots at the back: clear the hips (4–6)
D5 Low defence: blocked replies at the feet of the net player (3–5)
D6 One v two: lob-only defender against a net pair (4–6)
D7 Reset ball: slow high middle lob to buy time (3–5)
D8 Defend on your partner's voice (calls "glass", "out", "yours") (3–5)
D9 Ten-ball survival: defender keeps ten balls in play under pressure (4–6)
D10 Defending the x3 where out-of-court play is allowed (5–6)

## 06 Serve, Return & the First Four Shots (S, 8)
S1 Second-serve targets under pressure (3–5)
S2 Serve and first volley deep (3–5)
S3 Serve and first volley to the feet (4–6)
S4 Return lob over the server's partner, then join (3–5)
S5 Return low at the incoming server's feet (4–6)
S6 Server's partner poaches the return (5–6)
S7 Deciding-point serve plan (golden point: receivers choose the side) (4–6)
S8 First four shots game: points are scored by the pair that wins the first four shots (3–6)

## 08 Pressure Games (M, 12)
M1 Three lives (3–6)
M2 Earn the smash: smash allowed only after three bandejas in the rally (4–6)
M3 Glass first: back pair must use the glass before attacking (3–6)
M4 Second-serve match (4–6)
M5 Narrow court doubles with cone lanes (4–6)
M6 Net or nothing: only points won at the net count (3–6)
M7 Pair pressure ladder (club challenge format) (3–6)
M8 Condition cards: each game draws a condition (4–6)
M9 Stats match: count unforced errors and net points won (3–6)
M10 Hot seat: one player stays, partners rotate (3–6)
M11 Handicap start: games start at 15–30 or 0–30 (3–6)
M12 Deciding-point rehearsal: ten deuce points in each format (advantage, golden point) (4–6)
