 Welcome Welcome INTRODUCTION · WHY DRILLS Why drills beat matches. In a 90-minute match you hit around 250 to 300 balls. Maybe 15 of them are bandejas. In a 20-minute drill you can hit 60. Matches are where you test your game. Drills are where you build it. A match gives you one bajada every ten minutes, under pressure, with no time to think about what went wrong. A drill gives you twenty in a row, with a clear target and a partner or coach watching your feet. Players who only play matches stop improving at around level 3. They repeat the same habits, good and bad, every week. Players who drill twice for every three matches keep moving up the scale, because they practise the exact shots and decisions that cost them points. RULE OF THREE For every three matches you play, run one structured drill session. That ratio alone moves most club players up a level in a season. INTRODUCTION · HOW TO USE THIS BOOK How to use this book. 01 Pick your level Every drill shows a level range from the 0–7 level scale in Part 1. Train at your level and one above it. 02 Pick a focus Choose the chapter that fixes what is losing you points: the glass, the net, overheads, the return or positioning. 03 Build a 60-minute session Warm-up 10, technique 20, pattern 20, game 10. Part 1 shows the full session structure. 04 Score it Every drill has a simple score. Write it in the session log at the back and beat it next time. 05 Stay four weeks Run the same focus for four weeks before you change. Part 3 has ready-made plans. COACH’S CUE Two drills done well beat six done badly. Stop a drill when the quality drops, not when the clock says so. INTRODUCTION · READING THE DIAGRAMS Reading the diagrams. Before You Drill P1 Before You Drill WARM-UP · LEVELS · EQUIPMENT · FEEDING · SESSION PLAN Ten minutes of movement before the first ball saves you a month of calf strains. BEFORE YOU DRILL · WARM-UP Eight moves, ten minutes. 01 Easy jog, 2 laps of the court Inside the glass, both directions. Nose breathing. 02 Side shuffles, 2 × net to back glass Low hips, feet never cross. 03 Carioca, 2 × baseline width Crossover steps to open the hips. 04 Walking lunge with rotation, 10 Turn the chest over the front knee, racket in both hands. 05 Arm circles and shoulder swings, 20 Forward, back, then across the body. 06 Split-step jumps, 3 × 10 Small hop, land on the balls of the feet, knees soft. 07 Shadow strokes, 10 each Forehand, backhand, forehand volley, backhand volley, bandeja. 08 Three accelerations, net to back glass Build to 80% speed. Walk back. Then 3 minutes of mini-padel at the net before any full-length rally. BEFORE YOU DRILL · LEVELS The level scale. LEVEL PLAYER WHAT THEY CAN DO 0 First timer Learns grip and rules, rallies with the coach. 1 Beginner Rallies 5 balls, starts using the back glass. 2 Improving Volleys at the net, serves in, lobs with intent. 3 Club regular Exits the back glass, plays bandeja, holds the net. 4 Strong club Double walls, víbora, controls pace and depth. 5 Competitive Bajada, smash por 3, tactical patterns as a pair. 6 Advanced Wins regional events; few unforced errors. 7 Elite National or professional level. Most club players are 2–4. Every drill in this book shows its level range. BEFORE YOU DRILL · EQUIPMENT What you need. 01 A basket of 30–50 balls Old balls are fine for feeding. Use fresh balls for the game drills at the end of a session. 02 8–12 flat cones Flat discs don’t bounce balls away. Use two colours if you can. 03 Targets Towels, racket covers or hoops for the T1 and T2 zones. Anything that shows a 1 × 1 m area. 04 A timer Your phone on the bench, set to the drill time. 05 A pen and this book Write every score in the session log. What you measure, you improve. ON COURT Book a full hour, even for solo drills. A wall session on a quiet morning court is the cheapest training you will ever do. BEFORE YOU DRILL · FEEDING How to feed balls properly. A bad feed ruins a good drill. Feeding is a skill, and the feeder decides whether the drill trains anything. 01 Same ball, every ball For technique blocks, feed the same height, speed and spin 10 times in a row. 02 Feed from the right spot Stand where the real shot would come from: the opponent’s service line for volleys, behind the net post for lobs. 03 Rhythm One ball every 4–5 seconds for technique, every 2–3 seconds for pressure drills. 04 Hand feeds first Beginners start with underarm hand feeds. Move to racket feeds when 7 out of 10 land in. 05 Talk less One cue per set, said before the feed, not during the shot. BEFORE YOU DRILL · SESSION PLAN The 60-minute session. Solo & Wall Drills 01 Solo & Wall Drills TEN DRILLS · ONE PLAYER · A BASKET AND A WALL The back glass never misses. Use it as your hitting partner. SOLO & WALL · WHAT THIS CHAPTER FIXES What this chapter fixes. No partner, no coach, no excuse. The glass is the most patient hitting partner you will ever have. 01 Clean contact Hundreds of balls in 20 minutes groove the swing path and the contact point in front of the body. 02 Reading the rebound Solo glass drills teach you how fast and where the ball comes off the back and side walls. 03 Hands at the net Fast wall volleys sharpen reactions and the short, firm volley punch. 04 Serve accuracy A ladder of serve targets builds a serve you can trust at golden point. RULE OF THREE Three solo sessions of 30 minutes a fortnight will improve your contact more than three extra matches. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 01 Forehand wall rally. Level: 1–3 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Forehand contact, rhythm Stand 4 m from the back glass, forehand side, and rally against it. HOW IT WORKS 01 Set up. Stand 4 m from the back glass, side-on, racket back early. Drop the ball and hit an easy forehand into the glass at waist height. 02 Let it bounce. Let the rebound bounce once on the floor, then hit again. Aim for the same spot on the glass every time. 03 Count. Keep the rally going. Restart from a drop-feed when it breaks. Work in 2-minute sets with 30 seconds rest. COACH'S CUE Turn the shoulders first, then swing. Contact in front of the front foot, every ball. COMMON MISTAKE Standing too close: the ball jams you. Take one step back so it drops into your hitting zone. MAKE IT HARDER Alternate soft and medium pace without breaking the rally. MAKE IT EASIER Catch the rebound and restart each time to slow it down. SCORE IT Longest rally in 2 minutes. Beat 20, then 40. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 02 Backhand wall rally. Level: 1–3 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Backhand contact, balance Same set-up as Drill 1, now on the backhand, with the front shoulder to the glass. HOW IT WORKS 01 Grip and stance. Continental grip, front shoulder towards the glass, weight on the back foot. 02 Short swing. Hit with a compact, low-to-high swing. Finish with the racket head pointing at the glass. 03 Rhythm. Rally after one bounce. Three sets of 2 minutes, 30 seconds rest between. COACH'S CUE Keep the non-hitting hand on the throat of the racket until the swing starts. COMMON MISTAKE Opening the chest too early, which sprays the ball wide. Hold the side- on position through contact. MAKE IT HARDER Hit two forehands, then two backhands, without stopping. MAKE IT EASIER Use a bigger target on the glass and a slower ball. SCORE IT Count clean contacts in 2 minutes. Target: 25. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 03 Alternating wall rally. Level: 2–4 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Footwork, changing wings Forehand, then backhand, then forehand: the feet do the work, not the wrist. HOW IT WORKS 01 Start central. Stand in the middle of the court, 4 m from the glass. 02 Alternate. Hit a forehand to the glass on your right side, then a backhand on your left. Keep alternating. 03 Recover. After each shot take a small shuffle back to the centre before the next ball. 04 Sets. Four sets of 90 seconds. Rest 30 seconds. COACH'S CUE Split-step when the ball hits the glass, then move. Never wait flat-footed. COMMON MISTAKE Reaching with the arm instead of moving the feet. If you are stretching, you started late. MAKE IT HARDER Add a volley at the glass every fourth ball. MAKE IT EASIER Allow two bounces when the rally gets out of control. SCORE IT Alternating shots in a row without a miss. Target: 16. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 04 Back-glass feed and play. Level: 1–3 · Players: 1 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Back-glass exit timing Throw the ball against the glass yourself, let it come out and play it over the net. HOW IT WORKS 01 Self-feed. From 3 m in front of the back glass, throw the ball firmly at the floor so it bounces once and then hits the glass. 02 Turn and wait. Turn side-on to the glass and let the ball come out to you. Don’t chase it into the corner. 03 Play it. Hit a controlled forehand cross-court into T1 in the opponents’ half. 04 Repeat. 20 balls on the forehand, then 20 on the backhand side. COACH'S CUE Move away from the glass with the ball, not towards it. Give it space to drop. COMMON MISTAKE Hitting the ball as it comes off the glass at shoulder height. Wait until it drops to hip height. MAKE IT HARDER Play every exit down the line into a T2 at the opponents’ side glass. MAKE IT EASIER Throw the ball higher and softer so it comes out slowly. SCORE IT Exits into T1 out of 20. Target: 12. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 05 Side-glass corner exit. Level: 2–4 · Players: 1 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Reading the side glass The ball comes off the side glass first, then towards you. Read it and step back. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed into the side wall. From near the service line, throw the ball so it bounces and hits the side glass about 1 m from the corner. 02 Open up. Turn your chest towards the side glass and let the ball come off it. 03 Play deep. Hit a controlled ball deep into T1. Height over the net: about 1 m. 04 Switch sides. 15 balls on the forehand side, then 15 on the backhand side. COACH'S CUE Back foot behind the ball. If the ball is behind you, you have already lost the point. COMMON MISTAKE Rushing in towards the corner. The ball moves away from the side glass, so let it come to you. MAKE IT HARDER Feed harder so the ball also clips the back glass. MAKE IT EASIER Feed slower, from 2 m away, and catch the first few rebounds. SCORE IT Clean exits into T1 out of 15 per side. Target: 9. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 06 Bounce-and-catch control. Level: 0–2 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Racket control, touch Stay inside a 2 × 2 m box of four cones. HOW IT WORKS 01 Bounce up. Bounce the ball up off the strings to head height, 20 times without moving out of the box. 02 Bounce down. Dribble the ball on the floor with the racket, 20 times. 03 Catch. Bounce it up high, let it drop, and kill it dead on the strings without it bouncing off. 04 Edge and back. Alternate forehand and backhand faces for 20 touches. COACH'S CUE Soft hands. Let the racket give a little at contact, like catching an egg. COMMON MISTAKE Gripping the racket too hard, so the ball flies off. Hold at 4 out of 10 pressure. MAKE IT HARDER Do it while walking a slow lap of the court. MAKE IT EASIER Allow one floor bounce between each touch. SCORE IT Touches in a row without leaving the box. Target: 50. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 07 Rapid-fire volley wall. Level: 2–5 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Quick hands, short volley Volley against the back glass from 2 m, no bounce. HOW IT WORKS 01 Close up. Stand 2 m from the back glass with the racket up in front of your chest. 02 Volley. Volley the ball against the glass without letting it bounce. Short punch, no backswing. 03 Mix it. Ten forehand volleys, ten backhand volleys, then alternate. 04 Sets. Five sets of 45 seconds, 15 seconds rest. COACH'S CUE Racket head above the wrist, always. Block, don’t swing. COMMON MISTAKE Swinging at the ball. At 2 m there is no time; the face angle does the work. MAKE IT HARDER Step in to 1.5 m for faster exchanges. MAKE IT EASIER Step back to 3 m and allow a bounce when needed. SCORE IT Volleys in a row in 45 seconds. Target: 30. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 08 Bandeja shadow swings. Level: 2–4 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Overhead shape, footwork Move back from the net and swing without a ball. Shape first, then add balls. HOW IT WORKS 01 Start at the net. Stand 2 m from the net in a ready position. 02 Turn and move back. Turn side-on, shuffle back three steps with the racket up behind the head and the free arm pointing at an imaginary ball. 03 Swing. Make a bandeja swing: flat, slightly sliced, finishing across the body at chest height. Hold the finish for one second. 04 Recover. Shuffle forward to the net. Repeat 10 times on each side, 3 sets. MAKE IT HARDER Throw a ball up and hit a real bandeja into the glass from the service line. MAKE IT EASIER Do the swing standing still, no movement. SCORE IT Clean repetitions held in the finish. Target: 30 in 3 sets. COACH'S CUE Side-on before you swing. The free hand points at the ball until you hit. COMMON MISTAKE Running backwards facing the net. Turn the hips first, then shuffle. SOLO & WALL · DRILL 09 Solo serve target ladder. Level: 1–4 · Players: 1 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Serve accuracy, routine Serve from the right, bottom side. T1 at the centre, T2 at the side glass. HOW IT WORKS 01 Set the targets. Put a towel at the T (T1) and one 1 m from the side glass (T2) in the diagonal service box. 02 Routine. Bounce the ball twice, look at the target, serve. Same routine every time. 03 Ladder. Serve until you hit T1 three times, then move to T2. Then switch to the other box. 04 Under the waist. Contact at or below waist height, as the rules require. COACH'S CUE Look at the target before the serve, not at the ball after it. COMMON MISTAKE Serving hard and flat into the net. A deep, steady serve with slice wins more points. MAKE IT HARDER Every serve must bounce and hit the side glass after T2. MAKE IT EASIER Make the targets 1.5 × 1.5 m. SCORE IT Total serves needed to finish the ladder on both sides. Beat SOLO & WALL · DRILL 10 Racket dribble and balance. Level: 0–2 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Balance, coordination Dribble the ball forward from the back to the net and back. HOW IT WORKS 01 Walk and dribble. Dribble the ball on the floor with the racket while walking from the back cone to the net cone. 02 Turn. At the net, bounce the ball up on the strings 5 times, then walk back dribbling. 03 One leg. On the way back, stop every 2 m and bounce the ball up while standing on one leg for 5 touches. 04 Repeat. Six lengths. COACH'S CUE Eyes on the ball, knees soft. Balance comes from the legs, not the arm. COMMON MISTAKE Walking too fast and losing control. Slow down until you can do a full length without a miss. MAKE IT HARDER Do it sideways with a shuffle. MAKE IT EASIER Remove the one-leg balance stops. SCORE IT Lengths completed without the ball getting away. Target: 6 out SOLO & WALL · BUILD A SESSION A solo session. Groundstrokes & the Glass 02 Groundstrokes & the Glass FIFTEEN DRILLS · RALLY · EXITS · DOUBLE WALLS · DEFENCE Most points at club level are decided by what you do after the ball hits the back glass. GROUNDSTROKES · WHAT THIS CHAPTER FIXES What this chapter fixes. Club players lose more points at the back glass than anywhere else. Not because the shots are hard, but because they rush them. 01 Patience at the glass Letting the ball come off the wall and drop into your zone, instead of hitting it early and high. 02 Depth and direction Rallying cross-court and down the line with a target, not just over the net. 03 Low balls Driving and chiquitas that land at the net players’ feet and force them to volley up. 04 Defensive lobs Lobbing off the back glass when you are under pressure, deep enough to win back the net. RULE OF THREE From the back of the court, play three balls: one to survive, one to build, one to win back the net. Don’t try to win the point with the first. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 11 Cross-court rally. Level: 1–4 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Consistency, height Two players rally diagonally from the back of the court, each hitting into a deep target. HOW IT WORKS 01 Diagonal set-up. Both players start 1 m in front of the back glass, on opposite diagonals. 02 Rally. Rally cross-court, every ball landing in the deep target zone, 2–5 m from the back glass. 03 Height. Clear the net by 1–1.5 m. Use the glass when the ball gets past you. 04 Switch. After 4 minutes switch to the other diagonal. COACH'S CUE Hit through the ball towards the target, not up at the sky. Height comes from the swing path. COMMON MISTAKE Going for pace too early. Hold the rally at 60% speed until you can hit 20 in a row. MAKE IT HARDER Only balls that land in the target count as ‘in’. MAKE IT EASIER Play mini-padel inside the service boxes first. SCORE IT Longest rally landing in the targets. Target: 15. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 12 Down-the-line switch. Level: 2–4 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Direction change Rally cross-court; on the call, A changes direction down the line. HOW IT WORKS 01 Cross-court build. Start with three cross-court balls between A and B. 02 Change it. On the fourth ball A plays down the line, along the side glass, into T2. 03 Chase. B moves across and continues the rally cross-court from the other side. 04 Rotate. Swap roles every 10 rallies. COACH'S CUE Change direction with the feet and the contact point, not the wrist. Hit the line ball slightly later. COMMON MISTAKE Going down the line from a bad position. Only change direction when the ball is in your hitting zone. MAKE IT HARDER The change can come on any ball, chosen by A. B must react. MAKE IT EASIER Fix the change to the fourth ball every time. SCORE IT Down-the-line balls into T2 out of 10. Target: 6. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 13 Depth ladder. Level: 2–5 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Depth control T1 is the last 3 m before the back glass. T2 is the area just behind the service line. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed. F feeds medium-paced balls to A at the back of the court. 02 Climb the ladder. A plays 5 balls deep into T1, then 5 short into T2, then alternates T1 and T2. 03 Same swing. Change depth with swing length and racket angle, not with a different shot. 04 Swap. Swap roles after 30 balls. COACH'S CUE Deep is a longer, higher follow- through. Short is a shorter swing with an open face. Same preparation. COMMON MISTAKE Every ball landing in the middle of the court. Exaggerate: deeper than you think, shorter than you think. MAKE IT HARDER Call the target just before contact. MAKE IT EASIER Use a single deep target only. SCORE IT Balls in the called target out of 20. Target: 12. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 14 Back-glass exit. Level: 2–4 · Players: 2 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Patience, timing F feeds deep so the ball bounces and hits the back glass. A lets it come out and plays cross-court. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed deep. F feeds from the net so the ball bounces 2 m from the back glass and comes off it waist-high. 02 Turn early. A turns side-on to the back glass as soon as the ball passes the service line. 03 Move with the ball. Step forward with the ball as it comes off the glass. Hit when it has dropped to hip height. 04 Target. Play cross-court into T1. 15 balls forehand, 15 backhand. COACH'S CUE Turn, wait, step forward. Your first move is towards the net, not the glass. COMMON MISTAKE Backing into the corner and getting jammed. Keep 1.5 m between you and the glass. MAKE IT HARDER Feed with more pace and backspin so the ball sits lower off the glass. MAKE IT EASIER Feed high and slow; let A catch the first 5 balls off the glass. Exits into T1 out of 15 per side. Target: 10. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 15 Double-wall read: side then back. Level: 3–5 · Players: 2 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Reading two walls The ball hits the side glass first, then the back glass. A waits for the second wall. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed into the corner. F feeds hard into the backhand corner so the ball hits the side glass and then the back glass. 02 Read the angle. A follows the ball with the front shoulder. The second wall sends it back towards the middle. 03 Stay off the wall. Wait 1 m from the back glass and let the ball come to you. 04 Play it. Return cross-court and deep into T1. 12 balls, then switch to the forehand corner. COACH'S CUE The second wall is your friend. Wait for it and the ball comes back into the court. COMMON MISTAKE Hitting after the first wall, when the ball is still moving fast and away from you. MAKE IT HARDER F mixes single-wall and double-wall feeds without warning. MAKE IT EASIER Feed slower, so the ball dies after the second wall. SCORE IT Clean returns into T1 out of 12 per corner. Target: 7. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 16 Back then side exit. Level: 3–5 · Players: 2 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Back-side double wall The ball hits the back glass, then the side glass, and comes out along the wall. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed. F feeds so the ball bounces near the back glass close to the corner, then rebounds into the side glass. 02 Open to the side. A turns to face the side glass after the first rebound. 03 Follow it out. The ball runs out along the side glass. Move with it, keeping a racket-length from the wall. 04 Play. Lob or drive cross-court into T1. 12 per side. COACH'S CUE Watch the second bounce off the side glass, then take one step away from the wall before you hit. COMMON MISTAKE Trying to hit before the second wall. Be patient: this ball gets slower. MAKE IT HARDER Play the exit as a lob over a net player at the service line. MAKE IT EASIER Feed softer and allow the ball to bounce twice. SCORE IT Clean exits out of 12 per side. Target: 7. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 17 Low drive to the feet. Level: 2–4 · Players: 3 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Low, flat drive A drives low from the back to the feet of C, who stands at the net. HOW IT WORKS 01 Set up. C stands 2 m from the net on the opposite side. F feeds to A at the back of the court. 02 Drive low. A plays a flat drive that clears the net by 30–50 cm and dips to C’s feet. 03 C’s job. C only volleys if the ball is above the waist. Below the waist, C lets it go. 04 Rotate. Rotate roles after 15 balls. COACH'S CUE Low over the net, not hard. Take 20% off the pace and the ball dips. COMMON MISTAKE Hitting with pace and no spin. The ball sails at C’s chest and comes back at you. Add topspin. MAKE IT HARDER C now volleys every ball back to A’s feet. Rally continues. MAKE IT EASIER Play from the service line, not the back glass. SCORE IT Balls that reach C below the knee out of 15. Target: 8. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 18 Chiquita control. Level: 3–5 · Players: 3 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Soft low ball A plays a soft, slow chiquita that lands in the 2 m zone just past the net. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed. F feeds medium balls to A around the service line. 02 Touch. A plays a slow, low ball with an open face that lands in T1 and stays low. 03 Pressure. C stands 3 m from the net and must move forward and volley up. 04 Follow. After the chiquita, A takes two steps forward towards the net. Rotate after 15. COACH'S CUE Slow the racket down. A chiquita is a feel shot: short swing, soft hands. COMMON MISTAKE Playing it too high. If C can volley down, it was not a chiquita. Aim lower over the net. MAKE IT HARDER C volleys back and the point continues. MAKE IT EASIER Make T1 3 m deep. SCORE IT Chiquitas in T1 that C must volley up. Target: 9 out of 15. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 19 Lob off the back glass. Level: 2–5 · Players: 2 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Defensive lob depth A lets the ball come off the back glass and lobs it over the net players into the last 3 m. HOW IT WORKS 01 Feed to the glass. F feeds from the net so the ball comes off the back glass at waist height. 02 Open face. A opens the racket face and lifts the ball from low to high. 03 Height. The lob should peak above where the net players’ rackets could reach: about 6 m high. 04 Depth. Land in T1 between the service line and the back glass. 20 balls. COACH'S CUE Lift with the legs. Bend low before the shot and stand up through it. COMMON MISTAKE Short lobs that land at the service line. Aim for the back glass, not the service line. MAKE IT HARDER F stands at the net and tries to smash anything short. MAKE IT EASIER Lob from a hand feed without the glass. SCORE IT Lobs landing in T1 out of 20. Target: 12. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 20 The contra-pared. Level: 4–6 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Hitting into your own glass A is too late for a normal lob, so plays the ball into the own back glass to send it over the net. HOW IT WORKS 01 The situation. F feeds a fast, deep ball that passes A before it can be played normally. 02 Turn and hit back. A turns towards the back glass and hits the ball upwards into it, about 2 m high. 03 Over the net. The ball rebounds off A’s own glass in a lob over the net into T1. 04 Repeat. 12 balls on the forehand side, 12 on the backhand side. COACH'S CUE An emergency shot. Hit up and firm into the glass, not flat. COMMON MISTAKE Hitting the glass too low. The ball comes back into the net. Aim for 2 m up the glass. MAKE IT HARDER F volleys any short contra-pared back at A’s feet. MAKE IT EASIER F feeds slower and higher. SCORE IT Contra-pared that clear the net and land in T1 out of 12. Target: 5. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 21 Recover after the glass. Level: 2–4 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Recovery position After every exit, A recovers to the cone 3 m from the glass before the next feed. HOW IT WORKS 01 Exit. F feeds to the back glass. A plays the exit cross-court. 02 Recover. A moves forward to the recovery cone, 3 m from the back glass and 1 m inside the side line, and split-steps. 03 Next ball. F feeds the next ball only when A has split-stepped at the cone. 04 Sets. Sets of 8 balls, 4 sets, alternating forehand and backhand sides. COACH'S CUE The shot is not finished until your feet are back on the cone. COMMON MISTAKE Admiring the shot. The next ball arrives while you are still at the glass. MAKE IT HARDER F feeds as soon as A’s ball crosses the net, not when A is ready. MAKE IT EASIER Walk back to the cone; take a 5-second pause. SCORE IT Sets where A reaches the cone before every feed. Target: 4 out GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 22 Three-ball wall sequence. Level: 3–5 · Players: 2 · Time: 12 min · Focus: Linking glass shots Ball 1 back glass, ball 2 side glass, ball 3 back glass on the other wing. HOW IT WORKS 01 Ball one. F feeds deep to the back glass in the middle. A exits to any target. 02 Ball two. F immediately feeds to the side glass on the backhand side. A exits. 03 Ball three. F feeds to the back glass on the forehand side. A exits cross-court. 04 Rest and repeat. Rest 30 seconds after each sequence. 8 sequences. COACH'S CUE Reset your feet between every ball. Small steps, then turn. COMMON MISTAKE Using the same distance from the glass for every wall. Side glass needs more room than back glass. MAKE IT HARDER F changes the order of the three balls every sequence. MAKE IT EASIER Allow a 3-second pause between balls. SCORE IT Sequences with three clean exits. Target: 5 out of 8. GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 23 Hit the middle. Level: 2–4 · Players: 4 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Middle as a target A and B play every ball through the middle of C and D. HOW IT WORKS 01 Four at the back. Two v two, all four players starting at the back of the court. 02 Middle only. Rally with every ball aimed at the T1 zone between the two opponents. 03 Talk. The receiving pair must call ‘mine’ or ‘yours’ on every middle ball. 04 Score. Play to 7 points, then switch roles. COACH'S CUE The middle creates doubt. Aim between the opponents’ hips. COMMON MISTAKE Hitting the middle at pace from a bad position. The middle is about direction, not speed. MAKE IT HARDER Add one net player per side; middle balls must go low. MAKE IT EASIER Allow any ball that lands within 1 m of T1. SCORE IT Middle balls that force a mistake or a weak reply. Target: 4 GROUNDSTROKES & GLASS · DRILL 24 Net-clearance control. Level: 1–3 · Players: 2 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Height over the net Rally from the back of the court, each ball crossing the net 1–1.5 m high. HOW IT WORKS 01 Visual mark. Hang a towel or a ribbon on the net post at 1.5 m to show the height window. 02 Rally. A and B rally from the back of the court. Every ball must pass between the net tape and the mark. 03 Adjust. TWO VS TWO · DRILL 89 Fifteen-ball rally challenge. Level: 1–4 · Players: 4 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Consistency Cooperative rally of 15 balls, then the point is live. HOW IT WORKS 01 Cooperate. All four rally cooperatively for 15 balls. 02 Count aloud. Count every ball out loud. 03 Live. On ball 16 the point is live. 04 Reset. If the rally breaks before 15, restart at 1. 10 minutes. MAKE IT HARDER Cooperate for 25 balls. MAKE IT EASIER Cooperate for 8 balls. SCORE IT Rallies reaching 15 in 10 minutes. Target: 6. COACH'S CUE Keep the ball in the middle with medium height until 15. COMMON MISTAKE Speeding up at 12 or 13 because you are bored. Discipline is the drill. TWO VS TWO · DRILL 90 Tie-break simulation. Level: 2–6 · Players: 4 · Time: 15 min · Focus: Closing sets Play tie-breaks only. Each starts at 5–5 in the set. HOW IT WORKS 01 Score. Play only tie-breaks: first to 7, win by 2. 02 Serve. Normal tie-break serve rotation. 03 Change ends. Change ends every 6 points. 04 Best of three. Best of three tie-breaks. MAKE IT HARDER Start each tie-break at 3–5 down. MAKE IT EASIER First to 5. SCORE IT Tie-breaks won in the session. COACH'S CUE Win your serve points. In a tie-break, the safe serve is the right serve. COMMON MISTAKE Changing the plan at 5–5. Stay with what won you the first points. GAMES · BUILD A SESSION A games session. Train exactly that. FITNESS · WHAT THIS CHAPTER FIXES What this chapter fixes. A padel point lasts 8 to 15 seconds and covers a few metres in every direction. Long runs don’t train that. 01 First-step speed Agility and cone drills for 2–5 m bursts. 02 Braking Stopping in balance so the shot is stable. 03 Strength Legs and core that protect knees, back and shoulders. 04 Recovery Mobility after sessions to stay on court all season. RULE OF THREE Two short fitness blocks a week, 15 minutes each, plus a cool-down after every session. That is enough for most club players. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 91 Agility ladder for padel. Level: 1–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Foot speed A flat ladder from the back of the court towards the net. HOW IT WORKS 01 Two feet in. Run through with two feet in each square. 3 times. 02 Lateral in-out. Side-on: in, in, out, out along the ladder. 3 times each way. 03 Icky shuffle. In, in, out to the side. 3 times. 04 Finish. At the end of each pass, split-step and shadow a volley. MAKE IT HARDER Add a fed volley at the end of each pass. MAKE IT EASIER Walk through first. SCORE IT Clean passes without touching the ladder. Target: 9 out of 10. COACH'S CUE Light feet, quiet steps. If the ladder moves, you are stamping. COMMON MISTAKE Looking at the feet. Look ahead, as you would at the ball. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 92 Cone star drill. Level: 1–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Multi-direction bursts Centre cone and four corner cones, 3.5 m apart. HOW IT WORKS 01 Centre start. Start on the centre cone in a ready position. 02 To the cones. Move to each corner cone and back to the centre, shadowing the matching shot: volleys forward, exits back. 03 Order. Front right, back left, front left, back right. 04 Sets. 5 sets of one full star, 40 seconds rest. COACH'S CUE Always face the net. Turn the hips for back cones, never your back to the net. COMMON MISTAKE Rounding the turns. Plant the outside foot and push back. MAKE IT HARDER A partner points to a random cone each time. MAKE IT EASIER Walk the pattern. SCORE IT Time for one star. Target: under 20 seconds. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 93 Lateral band walks. Level: 1–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Hip strength Mini-band around the knees, walk sideways between two cones. HOW IT WORKS 01 Band. Mini-band just above the knees. 02 Athletic stance. Half-squat, back straight, knees pushed out against the band. 03 Walk. Step sideways 10 steps to the cone, then 10 back. 04 Sets. 3 sets each direction. MAKE IT HARDER Band around the ankles. MAKE IT EASIER No band. SCORE IT Full sets without letting the knees cave in. Target: 3 of 3. COACH'S CUE Keep the tension on the band the whole time. Knees never touch. COMMON MISTAKE Standing up as you tire. Stay at the same height. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 94 Braking and stopping. Level: 2–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Deceleration Accelerate to the cone, stop in balance and hold for two seconds. HOW IT WORKS 01 Sprint. Accelerate from the back of the court to the cone near the net. 02 Brake. Stop in two or three short steps, low, weight on the balls of the feet. 03 Hold. Freeze in a hitting position for two seconds. 04 Back. Walk back. 8 repetitions. MAKE IT HARDER Stop and shadow a volley without any wobble. MAKE IT EASIER Run at 60% speed. SCORE IT Clean stops without stepping out of the position. Target: 8 of 8. COACH'S CUE Short steps to stop. The last step is a little wider and lower. COMMON MISTAKE Stopping on straight legs. That is how knees get hurt. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 95 Overhead jump and recover. Level: 3–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Jump overhead, landing Move back, jump for an overhead, land and recover forward. HOW IT WORKS 01 Move back. From the net cone, shuffle back to the back cone. 02 Jump. Jump off the back foot and shadow a smash, scissor-kicking the legs. 03 Land. Land on the front foot, in balance. 04 Recover. Shuffle forward to the net cone. 3 sets of 6. MAKE IT HARDER Add a real lob from a feeder. MAKE IT EASIER Shadow without the jump. SCORE IT Balanced landings out of 18. Target: 16. COACH'S CUE Land soft and recover forward at once. The smash is not the end. COMMON MISTAKE Landing on the back foot and falling backwards. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 96 Medicine-ball rotations. Level: 1–6 · Players: 2 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Rotational power Side-on throws against a partner or the side glass with a 2–3 kg ball. HOW IT WORKS 01 Side-on. Stand side-on, 3 m from a partner or the side glass. 02 Rotate. Turn the hips and shoulders back with the ball at hip height. 03 Throw. Rotate forward and throw the ball across the body. 04 Sets. 3 sets of 8 throws each side. MAKE IT HARDER Use a heavier ball, 4 kg. MAKE IT EASIER Use a 1 kg ball or no ball. SCORE IT Clean throws with hip rotation. Target: 48. COACH'S CUE The hips start the throw, the arms finish it. Same chain as a drive. COMMON MISTAKE Throwing with the arms only. Turn the hips first. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 97 Court-sprint intervals. Level: 2–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Repeat-sprint fitness Back glass to net and back, short rest, repeat. HOW IT WORKS 01 Sprint. Sprint from the back glass to the net and back. About 18 m. 02 Rest. Rest 20 seconds. 03 Repeat. 8 sprints. 04 Sets. 2 sets, 2 minutes between sets. COACH'S CUE Fast but relaxed. Tension makes you slower. COMMON MISTAKE Going all out on the first sprint and dying by the fifth. Pace it so all 8 are the same. MAKE IT HARDER Add a split-step and shadow volley at the net. MAKE IT EASIER Rest 40 seconds. SCORE IT Time for sprint 1 and sprint 8. Target: under 0.5 seconds difference. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 98 Split-squat strength. Level: 1–6 · Players: 1 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Leg strength Off court or beside it: back foot on a bench, front foot forward. HOW IT WORKS 01 Set up. Back foot on a bench, front foot about 70 cm in front. 02 Lower. Lower slowly until the front thigh is parallel with the floor. 03 Drive up. Push up through the front heel. 04 Sets. 3 sets of 8 each leg. MAKE IT HARDER Hold dumbbells, 5–10 kg. MAKE IT EASIER Do it without the bench as a split squat. SCORE IT Clean reps each leg. Target: 24. COACH'S CUE Front knee over the middle of the foot. The torso stays tall. COMMON MISTAKE Front knee falling inwards. Push it out over the little toe. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 99 Core anti-rotation. Level: 1–6 · Players: 2 · Time: 8 min · Focus: Core stability Partner holds a resistance band; A presses it out and resists the pull. HOW IT WORKS 01 Band. B holds one end of a resistance band to the side of A. 02 Press. A holds the band at the chest with both hands, then presses it straight out. 03 Resist. Hold for 3 seconds without letting the body turn. 04 Sets. 3 sets of 8 each side. MAKE IT HARDER Stand on one leg. MAKE IT EASIER Use a lighter band. SCORE IT Reps without rotation. Target: 48. COACH'S CUE Ribs down, glutes tight. The body should not move at all. COMMON MISTAKE Leaning away from the band. Stand straight and resist. FITNESS & FOOTWORK · DRILL 100 Cool-down mobility. Level: 0–7 · Players: 1 · Time: 10 min · Focus: Recovery Ten minutes on court after every session. HOW IT WORKS 01 Calves and hamstrings. Wall calf stretch and seated hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each side. 02 Hips. Kneeling hip-flexor stretch and figure-four glute stretch, 30 seconds each. 03 Shoulder. Cross-body and sleeper stretch, 30 seconds each side. 04 Breathe. Two minutes lying down, slow breathing. COACH'S CUE Slow and easy. A cool-down is not a workout. COMMON MISTAKE Skipping it. Most overuse injuries in padel come from shoulders and calves left tight. MAKE IT HARDER Add 5 minutes of foam rolling. MAKE IT EASIER Do only calves and shoulders. Back glass The glass wall behind each team, 3 m of glass topped by 1 m of fence. Bajada An attacking overhead played after the ball comes off the back glass. Bandeja A flat, sliced overhead used to keep the net. Chiquita A soft, slow, low ball at the net players’ feet. Contra-pared Hitting the ball into your own back glass to send it over the net. Double wall A ball that hits two walls, side then back or back then side. Drive side The right-hand side of the court for each team. Exit Playing the ball after it has come off the glass. Fence The metal mesh sections of the walls. Rebounds are unpredictable. Golden point At deuce, one deciding point. The receivers choose who returns. Lob A high ball over the net players, ideally landing near the back glass. GLOSSARY · M – Z No-man’s- land The area around the service line, between defence and the net. Por 3 A smash that bounces and leaves the court over the 3 m side wall. Por 4 A smash that bounces and leaves the court over the 4 m back wall. Revés side The left-hand side of the court for each team. Service line The line 6.95 m from the net. Serves land between it and the net. Side glass The glass walls on each side of the court near the back. Split-step A small hop as the opponent hits, to be ready to move in any direction. T Where the centre line meets the service line. Víbora An overhead with heavy side spin, usually to the side glass. Volley A shot played before the ball bounces.
