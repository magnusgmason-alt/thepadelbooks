## padel-side-wall
- Ball in play off own walls after bounce; own glass usable, own mesh not → FIP Rules 12, 14b, 13l. Own shot hitting opponents' glass/mesh before bounce = point lost → FIP 13g.
- Cross-court balls hit side glass first; pace/spin/angle decide rebound; offensive side-glass angles "take months" → Padel Browser (glass walls).
- Side-then-back kicks to middle; back-then-side kicks up the court along the side wall → Tennis Warehouse (using the walls); matches /padel-back-glass.
- Opening/closing double wall, semi-open stance "more closed than back wall", ball reaches leg nearest side wall → Padel Addict.
- Don't turn as soon as ball hits back glass; contact in front with room from side glass → The Padel School (double glass without turn).
- Two options: take early close to glass or allow space; depends on height/speed/angle → Ely Padel Club (LTA ClubSpark).
- Side walls more viable for defence; "hit forwards" → Tennis Warehouse.

## padel-gancho
- Over non-dominant shoulder, higher contact, straight arm, aim glass/fence so ball dies deep, right side for balls over left shoulder → The Padel School + Padel Browser (agree).
- Variant definitions: Padel.FYI (defensive overhead, higher contact than bandeja), The Padel Weekly (softer overhead to hold net), Bounce (softer angled overhead). Disagreement stated in article.
- Mistakes (bent arm, backing up, winner, short, square shoulders) → Padel Browser.

## padel-half-volley
- Definition; more common in padel because play to feet; risk when advancing → Padel.FYI.
- Use only when necessary; flat or topspin; very short backswing; contact in front; targets (deep, middle, back where it came from, below waist; soft or lob in defence); let balls reach the wall when possible; one-on-one bounce game → Padel World Press.
- Split step, early short backswing, wide stance, chest low, contact in line with front hip → The Padel School (backhand half-volley).
- Stop and split as opponent is about to hit; avoid no man's land → The Padel School (when to move forward).

## padel-spin
- Slice bounces low and slow off ground and back wall → Padel.FYI glossary. Topspin smash speeds up off back wall; topspin on chiquitas → Padel.FYI (topspin).
- Flat volley rises higher off back glass; slice stays low; speed > spin → The Padel School (volleys slice or flat).
- Flat drops earlier, easier to disguise; slice gives time → The Padel School (hitting flat). Slice from the back best above chest height → The Padel School (best time to slice). Disagreement with "slice by default" coaching stated.
- Racket roughness made little difference across most angles; small test of three rackets → Tennis Warehouse University physics test (caveat included).
- Bandeja: relax wrist for slice; víbora: lateral topspin via quick pronation → Babolat hitting techniques (noted as differing from "side spin + slice" description used on our site).
- No HEAD/Babolat serve quotes kept (serve and topspin-brush lines rewritten as general coaching).

## padel-split-step
- Definition, land as opponent contacts, no split = flat-footed and late → The Padel School (footwork).
- Lighter/quicker than tennis; heels off ground; hop then move once direction known → The Padel School (how to move your feet). Note: same page also says "higher"; omitted to stay consistent with /padel-footwork-drills ("a couple of centimetres").
- Ready position: racket high at net, down at back; dropping racket when partner hits is an error → The Padel School (shot preparation).
- "Neither before nor after"; stop short, split, continue when moving forward; adult beginners can prioritise getting ball over → Padel Magazine (Julien Bondia).
- Tennis players taught it already do it → The Padel School (from tennis to padel).
- Two metres from net → existing /padel-volley.

## tennis-habits-that-hurt-your-padel
- Short sharp movements vs big swings; power comes back off glass; back glass biggest challenge, then a support; instinct to play every ball in front → LTA Padel (padel vs tennis).
- Loopy swings; wall avoidance; smash control; drop shots/winners too early; mindset continues attacking; strengths incl. power and split step; easier to mould new technique than change habits → The Padel School (from tennis to padel).
- Tennis rotates 4–5 grips; continental base; eastern forehand allowed from the back by some; continental only for first months → Padel Browser grip guide (disagreement stated).
- Serve underarm after bounce, at/below waist → FIP Rule 6.

## padel-etiquette
- 3-minute courtesy rally; 20 s between points; 90 s change of ends; Rule 6.11; 13k; Rule 11 hindrance (deliberate = point, involuntary = let, second = point); Rule 10b/10e lets; etiquette ban on hitting ball at opponents when not in play → FIP (per fip_rules.md + CORRECTIONS).
- Arrive 5 min early; rotate balls; no smashing in warm-up ("most commonly broken"); own faults; unsure double bounce → point to opponents; return balls by roll/underarm, not launched; no taunting/sarcastic clapping; tap rackets; leave promptly; thank organiser; "leave the court better than you found it" → Padel Browser etiquette.
- Never walk into active court; walk round, don't cross net; warm-ups not mini matches; replay disputed point at same score; talk after opponent hits, before own stroke → Red Bull etiquette.
- Net-cord acknowledgement is a custom, not a rule → Tennis.com ("apologies accepted"). No padel-specific source found; presented as a borrowed tennis custom.
- Americano habits consistent with /padel-americano (report results quickly, agree format aloud).

## what-age-can-kids-start-padel
- First strokes from age 3; ~300 g under 10, ~330 g over 10, ~340 g transition; too heavy/big raises injury risk → HEAD (kids rackets).
- U12 (7–12) and U18 brackets, later U8 (5–8); progress by ability; foam/low-compression balls; no line drills; mini-rallies 3–5 shots; 60 min standard, 90 min too long for under-12s; wall play exploratory; no unsupervised access, no running on wet courts → USPA junior programme guide (2026).
- 5–8 fun/mini-padel; 9–12 sweet spot; 30–45 min for 5–8, an hour 9–12; call "mine"; never run through glass; clinics → private lessons → junior events in year two → Padel Browser parents' guide.
- FIP Promises from 2026: U12, U14, U16, U18; continental circuits + global U18 circuit → FIP news + FIP Promises 2026 PDF.
- Three eye injuries needing surgery, smash up to 130 km/h, goggles, up to 90% risk reduction (June 2021) → University of Gothenburg.
- No junior-specific racket rules; strap compulsory → FIP + existing /best-padel-rackets-for-kids.
