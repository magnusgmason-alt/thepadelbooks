## padel-insoles
- Shock-absorbing insoles did not reduce any injury type; orthoses linked to lower overall injury risk, mainly stress fractures; 11 orthosis trials + 7 insole trials; much evidence from military -> EurekAlert release on BJSM meta-analysis (news-releases/820213). The 28%/41% figures were deliberately left out of the body (relative vs absolute not stated).
- Landorf 2006 RCT: 135 people, prefabricated vs custom vs sham; pain improved equally; small function benefit at 3 months gone at 12 -> AAFP POEM (afp/2006/1201/p1932a).
- Plantar fasciitis self-care (soft insoles/heel pads, cushioned shoes, rest/raise, stretches; see GP after 2 weeks or with tingling/numbness; diabetes; referral to physio/podiatrist) -> NHS 111 Wales.
- Knee OA: suggest not offering lateral wedge insoles (medial OA); can't recommend for/against shock-absorbing insoles or arch supports (very low-quality evidence); may consider shock-absorbing footwear -> RACGP guideline page. (NICE NG226 page could not be fetched; BAPO summary of the NICE evidence review seen but not cited.)
- Fitting steps (use old liner as template, align heel/toe, trace, cut, trial, press heel) -> Livewell Southwest NHS podiatry PDF.
- Running shoes built mainly for forward movement -> The Padel School padel shoes page.

## padel-shoe-soles
- Herringbone = "clay sole"; for sand-covered turf and clay; can be too grippy on hard courts; omni for less sand/indoor, wears faster; hybrid for multiple courts -> PadelShop sole guide.
- Omni: direct grip, indoor carpet / little sand, wears faster and slides less on hard/cement; herringbone clears sand, grip drops when pattern wears; hybrid layout (herringbone outside, omni inside) -> PadelPROShop.
- Omni ("Omni Court") designed for sand-filled carpet; both patterns work on all padel courts; tennis shoes designed for hard/grass/clay; running shoes/casual trainers worst choice; toe reinforcement -> Tennisnuts. Disagreement with PadelShop/PadelPROShop flagged in text.
- Deep herringbone useful on sandier surfaces; clay-court tennis shoes can work; check traction; running shoes forward-only -> The Padel School.
- Indoor guide: choose by surface not indoor/outdoor label; omni/flat for low-sand indoor -> PadelShop indoor vs outdoor.
- Humidity/wet glass in cold, late afternoon to early morning -> NOX temperature article.

## how-to-change-a-padel-overgrip
- 10-step process (remove old, tie cord, start at butt cap, even wraps, mark, cut straight across, finishing tape, band back) -> Total Padel.
- Overlap 1/8-1/4 inch, pull tight, start at bottom -> Wilson tennis overgrip guide (tennis source, used only for overlap/tension).
- Overlap sets thickness; tear softer grips by hand; change when worn, at least every two months -> HEAD.
- Overgrip absorbs moisture, protects base grip; 0.4-0.6 mm; "thin for control, thick for comfort"; tacky for dry hands, dry for sweaty; 4-6 sessions for club players; layering two -> Padel Market.
- Tacky default, dry for sweaty/humid, dry wears faster; 2-4 sessions frequent players; layering for larger hands -> Padel Browser overgrips 2026.
- Wrist cord compulsory -> FIP rules (via fip_rules.md; not added to sources list).

## carbon-vs-fiberglass-padel-racket
- Feel/power/forgiveness/durability table; carbon for advanced, fibreglass for beginners; comfort tied to soft core; 3K softest/18K stiffest view -> Justpadel.
- K = thousands of filaments per tow -> Easy Composites.
- "Higher K = stiffer" mostly wrong; 18K more elastic; K is top sheet only; layers/resin matter more; soft core + round shape for sensitive arms -> Padel Browser K article.
- 3K dry/direct, 12K tolerant, 18K firmer and more vibration; rigidity transmits vibration -> PadelPROShop K article.
- US price tiers ($60-120 fibreglass; $120-200 carbon appears; $200-300 12K/18K frames; $300-450+ carbon faces), 361 rackets, Sept 2026 -> Padel Browser cost US 2026.
- Beginners: round, soft core, forgiving materials; fibreglass softer/carbon stiffer -> The Padel School.

## soft-vs-hard-padel-racket
- Soft = more rebound on slow shots, less on hard; hard = max power on hard shots; soft helps from the back; soft reduces vibration/wrist strain -> Decathlon racket guide.
- Hard foam: more control/precision, less automatic power; beginners soft core; advanced harder cores -> The Padel School.
- Hard = precision/stability, responds to faster balls; soft for beginners and tennis elbow (absorbs shock); winter soft / summer hard -> Padel USA hard vs soft.
- EVA vs polyethylene; multi-density cores; foam harder in cold/softer in heat; Varlion W/S versions -> Padel USA foam.
- Cold hardens/heat softens; racket recommendations by temperature; two rackets; balls lose/gain pressure -> NOX.
- Soft/hard EVA and foam characteristics -> Justpadel.
- Disagreement noted: Padel USA frames hard as "more power"; Decathlon/Padel School qualify by swing speed.

## padel-eye-protection
- Jakobsson & Thörnland, Läkartidningen June 2021; "potential high-risk sport"; three cases, all surgery; most injuries mild; lasting impairment in some; ball ~ eye-socket opening; smash ~130 km/h; rebounds hard to judge; goggles/visor/face shield; up to 90% risk reduction; floorball precedent -> GU press release (verified via EurekAlert copy, GU URL itself not fetchable).
- Aiello et al., Eye 2026 (doi 10.1038/s41433-026-04447-8): Italy, convenience online survey; 583 analysed; 60 (10.3%) injured; corneal abrasion 32%, subconjunctival haemorrhage 22%, retinal detachment 8.3%; 6 surgeries; ball 92%, own racket 8.3%; weekly match frequency only independent predictor; 9.6% strongly agreed goggles important; self-reported, hypothesis-generating -> Tor Vergata repository PDF.
- ASTM F803 scope includes racket sports (padel not named) -> technickenormy.cz ASTM F803-25 summary (not in article sources list; Prevent Blindness cited instead).
- ISO 18527-2:2021 squash/racquetball/squash 57 + sports with similar hazards -> iso.org.
- EN 166: general eye protectors for industry, labs, education, DIY -> Open University OpenLearn appendix (not in sources list).
- Polycarbonate most impact-resistant; prescription glasses/sunglasses/occupational safety glasses inadequate -> Prevent Blindness.
- A&E/999 and 111 criteria; cold compress 10-20 min, first 1-2 days; don't rub/press; no ice on skin -> NHS black eye + NHS eye injuries.
- Ready position "about two metres from the net, racket at chest height" matches /padel-volley and /padel-equipment.
