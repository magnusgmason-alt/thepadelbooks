## Sports psychology / learning research
- Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer (1993) defined deliberate practice as effortful activity designed to improve performance, with feedback -> Macnamara & Maitra 2019 (RSOS, PMC6731745) + jimdavies.org summary of the 1993 paper.
- 2019 replication: best vs good violinists ~8,224 h vs 9,844 h practice alone by 18, not significantly different; core finding not replicated -> Macnamara & Maitra 2019.
- Deliberate practice explained ~18% of variance in sports performance -> Macnamara, Moreau & Hambrick 2016 meta-analysis (Perspectives on Psychological Science 11(3)).
- Pre-performance routines = "sequence of task-relevant thoughts and actions" (citing Moran 1996); linked to focus, less distraction, recalling desired states; supporting studies questioned on design -> Cotterill 2015, The Sport Psychologist.
- Slow voluntary breathing presented as reducing stress/anxiety in athletes; authors say evidence limited/inconclusive -> Migliaccio et al. 2023, Sports 11(5):103 (narrative review).
- Golden points in pro padel: 61.5% (men) / 59.4% (women) end in an error; both sexes make more errors than winners -> Escudero-Tena et al. 2022, Applied Sciences 12(16):8125.
- Video feedback seems to enhance motor learning in PE, mostly paired with verbal feedback; expert modelling sometimes better than self-modelling; combination best in one study -> Mödinger, Woll & Wagner 2022 systematic review.
- Pros cover ~11 m per point on average, often <8 m; lobs used from the back to gain the net -> Aspetar Sports Medicine Journal, "Performance in padel" (Ramón-Llin et al. cited there).

- Mental toughness = managing nerves, resetting after mistakes, good decisions; breath / eye contact / cue word between points; acknowledge-identify-move on; control the controllables; practise under mild pressure -> The Padel School, mental toughness.

## Outdoor / health / nutrition
- Wind: with wind at back hit with less power, facing it hit harder; extra footwork; more effect above glass height; sun: lob opponents facing it; heat: balls bouncier, glass livelier; cold: balls less responsive, topspin smashes may not reach back, volleys die near back glass; light rain on artificial grass not much more slippery, heavy rain likely; wet glass: flat balls slide down, wipe with towel; humidity on glass; play 5–10 min before match; patience outdoors -> The Padel School, tactics for outside + weather affecting padel.
- Heat exhaustion symptoms, cool/drink/fan, recover within 30 min, heatstroke emergency signs; isotonic drink helps replace salt -> NHS heat exhaustion and heatstroke.
- SPF 30+, 4-star UVA, reapply every 2 h and after sweating/towel drying; shade 11am–3pm Mar–Oct (UK); sunglasses standards -> NHS sunscreen and sun safety.
- Start exercise hydrated; avoid >2% body-weight loss; individualise -> ACSM position stand, exercise and fluid replacement (PubMed 17277604).
- 1–4 g/kg carbohydrate 1–4 h before sessions >60 min; 200–300 kcal carb snack 30–60 min before -> HPRC nutrient timing.
- Stanford women's tennis (n=5) sleep extension to ~10 h: serves 12.6 -> 15.61, sprint 19.12 -> 17.56 s; preliminary -> AASM press release.
- Premier Padel 2026 viewing: rounds 1–3 on Premier Padel YouTube in most territories; QF–final on Red Bull TV and/or regional broadcasters (UK/IE Sky Sports + Red Bull TV; Spain Movistar+ + Red Bull TV; France Canal+; Italy Sky Italia; US/Canada beIN + Red Bull TV) -> Padel Speak 2026 guide (updated 15 Sep 2026). Premier Padel "Where to Watch" page lists broadcaster per event by country; YouTube has highlights/press conferences -> Padel Browser (May 2026). Article tells readers to check the official site.
